# The Robert Tilford Quran — 34 Translations

**By Robert Tilford (Hays, Kansas).**

The complete published Ayah Companion Quran translation collection: the Quran-alone English source — 114 chapters, 6,236 verses, audited and corrected — and 33 daughter translations made from it. Every translation in this collection was published and byte-verified on the author's archive.org shelf before being gathered here.

## Rights

These translations are **freely given; copies welcomed**. Attribution to Robert Tilford is requested. Translator rights are retained. If a corporation ever tries to claim one of these, the author's prior art — this repository and the archive.org shelf below — answers.

**Quran-alone method:** terms are defined by the Quran itself, never by hadith or tradition.

## Provenance

The original byte-verified shelf lives on archive.org under the identifiers in the table above (e.g. `https://archive.org/details/ayah-companion-tilford-welsh-manuscript`). This repository is the author's own mirror — a house nobody can lock.
