# THE I CHING

## The Book of Changes — The Core Text

**Translated by Robert Tilford**

Hays, Kansas · 2026

---

## About This Edition

This edition gives the core text of the *I Ching* — the 450 statement units of the sixty-four hexagrams: the judgment of each hexagram, its six line statements, and the two "all lines" statements that close the first two hexagrams. The commentaries known as the Ten Wings, which later tradition bound into the book, are not part of this edition; what stands here is the ancient core, whole and in order.

The Chinese text printed above each rendering is the received text. The English aims at plain, clear language throughout — the words of the oracle carried across as directly as they will go. James Legge's translation of 1899 was kept beside the work as the standing comparison, and the rendering departs from his only where the Chinese was judged to read otherwise.

Textual consultation and variant-reading cross-checks on flagged passages were assisted by DeepSeek; all translation decisions and final renderings are the translator's.

## Hexagram 1 — 乾 (Qián)

### The Judgment

乾，元亨利貞。

**Qián. The creative. Supreme success; it is good to hold fast.**

初九，潛龍勿用。

**First nine: A dragon hidden below. Do not act.**

九二，見龍在田，利見大人。

**Nine in the second: A dragon shows in the field. It is good to see the great man.**

九三，君子終日乾乾，夕惕若，厲无咎。

**Nine in the third: The noble man works at it all day long; at dusk he is still wary, as if in danger. Danger — but no blame.**

九四，或躍在淵，无咎。

**Nine in the fourth: Perhaps a leap — still in the deep pool. No blame.**

九五，飛龍在天，利見大人。

**Nine in the fifth: A dragon flying in the sky. It is good to see the great man.**

上九，亢龍有悔。

**Nine at the top: A dragon flown too high. There will be regret.**

### The Yong Line

用九，見群龍无首，吉。

**For all nines: You see a flight of dragons, with none at the head. Good fortune.**

## Hexagram 2 — 坤 (Kūn)

### The Judgment

坤，元亨，利牝馬之貞。君子有攸往，先迷後得，主利，西南得朋，東北喪朋，安貞吉。

**Kūn. The receptive. Supreme success. It is good to hold fast like a mare. The noble man has somewhere to go: if he goes first, he loses the way; if he follows, he finds his guide — and that is the gain. In the southwest, friends are found; in the northeast, friends are lost. Quiet holding fast brings good fortune.**

初六，履霜，堅冰至。

**First six: Treading on frost — hard ice is on its way.**

六二，直方大，不習无不利。

**Six in the second: It is straight, square, and great. Without practice, all goes well.**

六三，含章可貞，或從王事，无成有終。

**Six in the third: Keep your pattern folded away inside; you may hold fast. Should you follow the king's service: nothing is finished in your name, yet the work comes to its end.**

六四，括囊，无咎无譽。

**Six in the fourth: A sack tied shut. No blame, no praise.**

六五，黃裳，元吉。

**Six in the fifth: A yellow lower garment. Supreme good fortune.**

上六，龍戰于野，其血玄黃。

**Six at the top: Dragons fight out in the wilds; their blood is black and yellow.**

### The Yong Line

用六，利永貞。

**For all sixes: It is good to hold fast, always.**

## Hexagram 3 — 屯 (Zhùn)

### The Judgment

屯，元亨利貞，勿用有攸往，利建侯。

**Zhùn. Sprouting. Supreme success; it is good to hold fast. Do not set out. It is good to set up lords.**

初九，磐桓，利居貞，利建侯。

**First nine: Lingering, pacing in place. It is good to hold fast where you are. It is good to set up lords.**

六二，屯如邅如，乘馬班如，匪寇婚媾，女子貞不字，十年乃字。

**Six in the second: Difficulty upon difficulty; the horses wheel and turn back. Not raiders — they come seeking marriage. The maiden holds fast and does not promise; after ten years, she promises.**

六三，即鹿无虞，惟入于林中。君子幾不如舍。往吝。

**Six in the third: Chasing a deer with no forester, you only push on into the forest. The noble man sees how it will turn out: better to let it go. Going on: humiliation.**

六四，乘馬班如，求婚媾，往，吉无不利。

**Six in the fourth: The horses wheel and turn back. To seek the marriage match, go: good fortune, all goes well.**

九五，屯其膏，小貞吉，大貞凶。

**Nine in the fifth: The bounty is pent up. On a small scale, hold fast: good fortune. On a great scale, hold fast: misfortune.**

上六，乘馬班如，泣血漣如。

**Six at the top: The horses wheel and turn back. Tears of blood, streaming down.**

## Hexagram 4 — 蒙 (Méng)

### The Judgment

蒙，亨。匪我求童蒙，童蒙求我。初筮告，再三瀆，瀆則不告。利貞。

**Méng. The young fool. Success. It is not I who seek the young fool; the young fool seeks me. The first casting informs. A second and third casting are an insult, and what insults is not answered. It is good to hold fast.**

初六，發蒙。利用刑人，用說桎梏，以往吝。

**First six: Dispelling the ignorance. It is good to use punishments — and with them, to strike off fetters and shackles. To go on beyond that: humiliation.**

九二，包蒙，吉。納婦吉，子克家。

**Nine in the second: Bearing with the ignorant: good fortune. Taking a wife: good fortune. The son is able to keep the house.**

六三，勿用取女，見金夫，不有躬，无攸利。

**Six in the third: Do not take this woman in marriage. She sees a rich man and no longer holds herself. No journey is good.**

六四，困蒙，吝。

**Six in the fourth: Hemmed in by ignorance: humiliation.**

六五，童蒙，吉。

**Six in the fifth: The young fool: good fortune.**

上九，擊蒙，不利為寇，利禦寇。

**Nine at the top: Striking at the ignorance. It is not good to be the raider; it is good to ward off raiders.**

## Hexagram 5 — 需 (Xū)

### The Judgment

需，有孚，光亨，貞吉，利涉大川。

**Xū. Waiting. There is trust. Bright success. Hold fast: good fortune. It is good to cross the great river.**

初九，需于郊，利用恒，无咎。

**First nine: Waiting out in the open fields. It is good to hold to what is constant. No blame.**

九二，需于沙，小有言，終吉。

**Nine in the second: Waiting on the sand. There is a little talk. In the end, good fortune.**

九三，需于泥，致寇至。

**Nine in the third: Waiting in the mud. It draws raiders down on you.**

六四，需于血，出自穴。

**Six in the fourth: Waiting in the blood. He comes out of the pit.**

九五，需于酒食，貞吉。

**Nine in the fifth: Waiting amid wine and food. Hold fast: good fortune.**

上六，入于穴，有不速之客三人來，敬之，終吉。

**Six at the top: He enters the pit. Three guests come uninvited; honour them. In the end, good fortune.**

## Hexagram 6 — 訟 (Sòng)

### The Judgment

訟，有孚窒惕，中吉終凶。利見大人，不利涉大川。

**Sòng. Contention. There is trust, but it is blocked, and fear stands in it. To stop in the middle: good fortune. To press through to the end: misfortune. It is good to see the great man. It is not good to cross the great river.**

初六，不永所事，小有言，終吉。

**First six: Do not drag the affair out. There is a little talk. In the end, good fortune.**

九二，不克訟，歸而逋其邑，人三百戶，无眚。

**Nine in the second: The suit cannot be won. He goes home and slips away into his own town — a town of three hundred households. No harm.**

六三，食舊德，貞厲，終吉，或從王事，无成。

**Six in the third: Living on old merit. Holding fast: danger — yet in the end, good fortune. Should you follow the king's service: nothing finished in your name.**

九四，不克訟，復即命渝，安貞吉。

**Nine in the fourth: The suit cannot be won. He comes back, submits to the decree, and changes course. Quiet holding fast brings good fortune.**

九五，訟，元吉。

**Nine in the fifth: Contention: supreme good fortune.**

上九，或錫之鞶帶，終朝三褫之。

**Nine at the top: Even if the broad belt of honour is bestowed on him, before one morning is out it will be stripped from him three times.**

## Hexagram 7 — 師 (Shī)

### The Judgment

師貞，丈人吉，无咎。

**Shī. The host. Hold fast. For the elder: good fortune. No blame.**

初六，師出以律，否臧凶。

**First six: The host sets out under discipline. If the order is not good: misfortune.**

九二，在師中吉，无咎，王三錫命。

**Nine in the second: In the middle of the army: good fortune. No blame. The king three times bestows the commission.**

六三，師或輿尸，凶。

**Six in the third: The host may haul corpses. Misfortune.**

六四，師左次，无咎。

**Six in the fourth: The host falls back and takes its camp. No blame.**

六五，田有禽，利執言，无咎。長子帥師，弟子輿尸，貞凶。

**Six in the fifth: There is game in the field. It is good to take up the command. No blame. The eldest son leads the host; the younger brother hauls corpses. Hold fast: misfortune.**

上六，大君有命，開國承家，小人勿用。

**Six at the top: The great lord holds the mandate. He founds states and settles houses. The small man is not to be used.**

## Hexagram 8 — 比 (Bǐ)

### The Judgment

比吉。原筮元永貞，无咎。不寧方來，後夫凶。

**Bǐ. Holding together. Good fortune. The original divination: supreme, enduring, hold fast. No blame. The restless come in from every side. The man who comes late: misfortune.**

初六，有孚，比之无咎。有孚盈缶，終來，有它吉。

**First six: There is trust. Joining with it: no blame. Trust fills the earthen jar to the brim; in the end there comes a good fortune beyond it.**

六二，比之自內，貞吉。

**Six in the second: Joining from within. Hold fast: good fortune.**

六三，比之匪人。

**Six in the third: Joining with those who are not the right people.**

六四，外比之，貞吉。

**Six in the fourth: Joining outward. Hold fast: good fortune.**

九五，顯比，王用三驅，失前禽，邑人不誡，吉。

**Nine in the fifth: Joining in the open. The king works the three-sided drive and lets the game that runs out ahead go free. The townspeople are not warned beforehand. Good fortune.**

上六，比之无首，凶。

**Six at the top: Joining with no head to it: misfortune.**

## Hexagram 9 — 小畜 (Xiǎoxù)

### The Judgment

小畜，亨。密雲不雨，自我西郊。

**Xiǎoxù. Small accumulation. Success. Dense clouds, no rain, from our western outskirts.**

初九，復自道，何其咎，吉。

**First nine: He returns by his own road. What blame could there be? Good fortune.**

九二，牽復，吉。

**Nine in the second: Drawn back together: good fortune.**

九三，輿說輻，夫妻反目。

**Nine in the third: The cart sheds its spokes. Husband and wife glare at one another.**

六四，有孚，血去惕出，无咎。

**Six in the fourth: There is trust. The blood departs; the fear goes out. No blame.**

九五，有孚攣如，富以其鄰。

**Nine in the fifth: There is trust, bound fast as by a cord. He is rich together with his neighbours.**

上九，既雨既處，尚德載，婦貞厲。月幾望，君子征凶。

**Nine at the top: The rain has come; the settling has come. Honour the virtue that bears the load. For the wife, holding fast: danger. The moon nearly full: for the noble man to march out, misfortune.**

## Hexagram 10 — 履 (Lǚ)

### The Judgment

履虎尾，不咥人，亨。

**Lǚ. Treading. Treading on the tiger's tail; it does not bite the man. Success.**

初九，素履，往无咎。

**First nine: Treading in plainness. Going on: no blame.**

九二，履道坦坦，幽人貞吉。

**Nine in the second: The road he treads is smooth and level. For the withdrawn man, hold fast: good fortune.**

六三，眇能視，跛能履，履虎尾，咥人凶。武人為于大君。

**Six in the third: The one-eyed man thinks he can see; the lame man thinks he can tread. He treads on the tiger's tail, and it bites the man: misfortune. The fighting man plays at being the great lord.**

九四，履虎尾，愬愬，終吉。

**Nine in the fourth: Treading on the tiger's tail, trembling, trembling. In the end, good fortune.**

九五，夬履，貞厲。

**Nine in the fifth: Resolute treading. Holding fast: danger.**

上九，視履，考祥其旋，元吉。

**Nine at the top: Watch the treading; examine the signs as you turn back. Supreme good fortune.**

## Hexagram 11 — 泰 (Tài)

### The Judgment

泰，小往大來，吉亨。

**Tài. Peace. The small goes away; the great comes. Good fortune; success.**

初九，拔茅茹，以其彙，征吉。

**First nine: Pulling up the couch grass by its roots, with its whole clump. Setting out: good fortune.**

九二，包荒，用馮河，不遐遺，朋亡，得尚于中行。

**Nine in the second: He takes in the wild and waste land. He crosses the river without a boat. He does not leave the far ones behind. Companions fall away. He is honoured for the middle road he walks.**

九三，无平不陂，无往不復。艱貞无咎。勿恤其孚，于食有福。

**Nine in the third: No level that does not slope; no going that does not come back. Hold fast in the hardship: no blame. Do not grieve over its trust. In his eating there is blessing.**

六四，翩翩，不富以其鄰，不戒以孚。

**Six in the fourth: Fluttering down, fluttering down. Not rich, but with his neighbours. No guard set — because of trust.**

六五，帝乙歸妹，以祉元吉。

**Six in the fifth: Di Yi gives the sister in marriage. By the blessing: supreme good fortune.**

上六，城復于隍，勿用師。自邑告命，貞吝。

**Six at the top: The city wall falls back into the moat. Do not use the army. The decree is proclaimed from his own town. Holding fast: humiliation.**

## Hexagram 12 — 否 (Pǐ)

### The Judgment

否之匪人，不利君子貞，大往小來。

**Pǐ. Blocked. This blockage is not men's doing. It is not good for the noble man to hold fast. The great goes away; the small comes.**

初六，拔茅茹，以其彙，貞吉，亨。

**First six: Pulling up the couch grass by its roots, with its whole clump. Hold fast: good fortune. Success.**

六二，包承，小人吉，大人否亨。

**Six in the second: Wrapped up in the duties laid on him. For the small man: good fortune. For the great man: blockage — and success through it.**

六三，包羞。

**Six in the third: Wrapped in shame.**

九四，有命无咎，疇離祉。

**Nine in the fourth: There is a decree. No blame. His fellows share the blessing.**

九五，休否，大人吉。其亡其亡，繫于苞桑。

**Nine in the fifth: The blockage comes to rest. For the great man: good fortune. "It will perish, it will perish" — tied to the mulberry clump.**

上九，傾否，先否後喜。

**Nine at the top: The blockage topples. First blockage, then joy.**

## Hexagram 13 — 同人 (Tóngrén)

### The Judgment

同人于野，亨。利涉大川，利君子貞。

**Tóngrén. Fellowship out in the open country. Success. It is good to cross the great river. It is good for the noble man to hold fast.**

初九，同人于門，无咎。

**First nine: Fellowship at the gate. No blame.**

六二，同人于宗，吝。

**Six in the second: Fellowship within the clan only. Humiliation.**

九三，伏戎于莽，升其高陵，三歲不興。

**Nine in the third: Troops hidden in the thicket. He climbs his high mound. Three years, and he does not rise.**

九四，乘其墉，弗克攻，吉。

**Nine in the fourth: He mounts their wall, but cannot make the attack. Good fortune.**

九五，同人，先號咷而後笑，大師克相遇。

**Nine in the fifth: In fellowship: first weeping and wailing, then laughter. The great host prevails, and they meet.**

上九，同人于郊，无悔。

**Nine at the top: Fellowship out in the open fields. No regret.**

## Hexagram 14 — 大有 (Dàyǒu)

### The Judgment

大有，元亨。

**Dàyǒu. Great possession. Supreme success.**

初九，无交害，匪咎，艱則无咎。

**First nine: No dealings with harm — and that is no fault. Keep to the hardship: no blame.**

九二，大車以載，有攸往，无咎。

**Nine in the second: A great cart to carry the load. Having somewhere to go: no blame.**

九三，公用亨于天子，小人弗克。

**Nine in the third: The lord makes his offering before the Son of Heaven. The small man cannot do it.**

九四，匪其彭，无咎。

**Nine in the fourth: He does not display all he has. No blame.**

六五，厥孚交如，威如，吉。

**Six in the fifth: His trust meets others in the open; dignity stands with it. Good fortune.**

上九，自天祐之，吉无不利。

**Nine at the top: Blessed from Heaven itself. Good fortune; all goes well.**

## Hexagram 15 — 謙 (Qiān)

### The Judgment

謙，亨，君子有終。

**Qiān. Modesty. Success. The noble man brings things to their end.**

初六，謙謙君子，用涉大川，吉。

**First six: Modest and again modest — the noble man. By it he crosses the great river: good fortune.**

六二，鳴謙，貞吉。

**Six in the second: Modesty that makes itself heard. Hold fast: good fortune.**

九三，勞謙，君子有終，吉。

**Nine in the third: Modesty that has laboured. The noble man brings it to its end. Good fortune.**

六四，无不利，撝謙。

**Six in the fourth: All goes well. Modesty spread abroad.**

六五，不富以其鄰，利用侵伐，无不利。

**Six in the fifth: Not rich, but with his neighbours. It is good to use invasion and attack. All goes well.**

上六，鳴謙，利用行師，征邑國。

**Six at the top: Modesty that makes itself heard. It is good to move the host, to march on towns and states.**

## Hexagram 16 — 豫 (Yù)

### The Judgment

豫，利建侯、行師。

**Yù. Contentment. It is good to set up lords and to set the host moving.**

初六，鳴豫，凶。

**First six: Contentment that cries itself out. Misfortune.**

六二，介于石，不終日，貞吉。

**Six in the second: Set fast as stone. Not waiting out the day. Hold fast: good fortune.**

六三，盱豫，悔，遲有悔。

**Six in the third: Eyes raised in contentment. Regret. If he lingers, there will be regret.**

九四，由豫，大有得。勿疑，朋盍簪。

**Nine in the fourth: From him the contentment flows. Great gain. Do not doubt it: friends gather, as hair gathers to the pin.**

六五，貞疾，恒不死。

**Six in the fifth: A settled sickness. Constant through it — and he does not die.**

上六，冥豫，成有渝，无咎。

**Six at the top: Contentment in the dark. What is completed will change. No blame.**

## Hexagram 17 — 隨 (Suí)

### The Judgment

隨，元亨利貞，无咎。

**Suí. Following. Supreme success; it is good to hold fast. No blame.**

初九，官有渝，貞吉。出門交有功。

**First nine: What he is charged with changes. Hold fast: good fortune. Out the gate, his dealings bear fruit.**

六二，係小子，失丈夫。

**Six in the second: Tied to the little boy, she loses the grown man.**

六三，係丈夫，失小子。隨有求得，利居貞。

**Six in the third: Tied to the grown man, she loses the little boy. Following, she gets what she seeks. It is good to hold fast where you are.**

九四，隨有獲，貞凶。有孚，在道以明，何咎？

**Nine in the fourth: Following brings a catch. Hold fast: misfortune. There is trust — held bright on the road — what blame could there be?**

九五，孚于嘉，吉。

**Nine in the fifth: Trust set upon the good. Good fortune.**

上六，拘係之，乃從維之，王用亨于西山。

**Six at the top: Held fast, bound fast, and after that bound with cords. The king makes his offering on the western mountain.**

## Hexagram 18 — 蠱 (Gǔ)

### The Judgment

蠱，元亨，利涉大川。先甲三日，後甲三日。

**Gǔ. The spoiling. Supreme success. It is good to cross the great river. For three days before the first day, prepare; for three days after it, settle.**

初六，幹父之蠱。有子，考无咎，厲，終吉。

**First six: Setting right what the father left spoiled. There is a son: the dead father carries no blame. Danger; in the end, good fortune.**

九二，幹母之蠱，不可貞。

**Nine in the second: Setting right what the mother left spoiled. He may not hold fast.**

九三，幹父之蠱，小有悔，无大咎。

**Nine in the third: Setting right what the father left spoiled. A little regret. No great blame.**

六四，裕父之蠱，往見吝。

**Six in the fourth: Letting the father's spoiling lie. Going on, he meets humiliation.**

六五，幹父之蠱，用譽。

**Six in the fifth: Setting right what the father left spoiled. He meets with praise.**

上九，不事王侯，高尚其事。

**Nine at the top: He does not serve kings or lords. He sets his own work high.**

## Hexagram 19 — 臨 (Lín)

### The Judgment

臨，元亨利貞，至于八月有凶。

**Lín. Approach. Supreme success; it is good to hold fast. By the eighth month, there will be misfortune.**

初九，咸臨，貞吉。

**First nine: Approaching, all together. Hold fast: good fortune.**

九二，咸臨，吉，无不利。

**Nine in the second: Approaching, all together. Good fortune; all goes well.**

六三，甘臨，无攸利，既憂之，无咎。

**Six in the third: A sweet approach. No journey is good. Once he is troubled over it: no blame.**

六四，至臨，无咎。

**Six in the fourth: The approach arrived in full. No blame.**

六五，知臨，大君之宜，吉。

**Six in the fifth: A knowing approach — what befits the great lord. Good fortune.**

上六，敦臨，吉，无咎。

**Six at the top: An earnest approach. Good fortune. No blame.**

## Hexagram 20 — 觀 (Guān)

### The Judgment

觀，盥而不薦，有孚顒若。

**Guān. Viewing. The hands are washed, but the offering is not yet presented. There is trust, solemn and reverent.**

初六，童觀，小人无咎，君子吝。

**First six: A child's view. For the small man: no blame. For the noble man: humiliation.**

六二，闚觀，利女貞。

**Six in the second: A view through the crack of the door. It is good for a woman to hold fast.**

六三，觀我生，進退。

**Six in the third: Viewing my own life: advance or withdraw.**

六四，觀國之光，利用賓于王。

**Six in the fourth: Viewing the glory of the state. It is good to be a guest at the king's court.**

九五，觀我生，君子无咎。

**Nine in the fifth: Viewing my own life. The noble man: no blame.**

上九，觀其生，君子无咎。

**Nine at the top: Viewing his life. The noble man: no blame.**

## Hexagram 21 — 噬嗑 (Shìkè)

### The Judgment

噬嗑，亨，利用獄。

**Shìkè. Biting through. Success. It is good to use the law-courts.**

初九，屨校滅趾，无咎。

**First nine: His feet are set in the stocks, and his toes are covered. No blame.**

六二，噬膚，滅鼻，无咎。

## Hexagram 62 — 小過 (Xiǎo Guò)

### The Judgment

小過，亨，利貞。可小事，不可大事。飛鳥遺之音，不宜上，宜下。大吉。

**Xiao Guo. Success. It is good to hold fast. Small things may be done; great things may not be done. A flying bird leaves its note behind: it is not fitting to go up, it is fitting to go down. Great good fortune.**

初六，飛鳥以凶。

**First six: A flying bird brings misfortune.**

六二，過其祖，遇其妣，不及其君，遇其臣，无咎。

**Six in the second: He passes by his grandfather and meets his grandmother; he does not reach the lord, and meets his minister. No blame.**

九三，弗過防之，從或戕之，凶。

**Nine in the third: He does not pass by, but must guard against it. If he follows, someone may wound him. Misfortune.**

九四，无咎，弗過遇之，往厲必戒，勿用永貞。

**Nine in the fourth: No blame. He meets it without passing by. Going brings danger; be on guard. Do not hold fast always.**

六五，密雲不雨，自我西郊，公弋取彼在穴。

**Six in the fifth: Dense clouds, no rain, from our western outskirts. The lord shoots and takes the bird in its cave.**

上六，弗遇過之，飛鳥離之，凶，是謂災眚。

**Six at the top: He does not meet it, but passes it by. The flying bird leaves him. Misfortune. This is called calamity and self-made injury.**

## Hexagram 63 — 既濟 (Jì Jì)

### The Judgment

既濟，亨小，利貞。初吉終亂。

**Ji Ji. Success in small matters. It is good to hold fast. Good fortune at the beginning, disorder at the end.**

初九，曳其輪，濡其尾，无咎。

**First nine: He drags back his wheel, and wets his tail. No blame.**

六二，婦喪其茀，勿逐，七日得。

**Six in the second: A woman loses her carriage-screen. Do not chase it. In seven days it will be found.**

九三，高宗伐鬼方，三年克之，小人勿用。

**Nine in the third: Gao Zong attacked the Demon Region and subdued it in three years. Small men must not be used.**

六四，繻有衣袽，終日戒。

**Six in the fourth: There are rags to plug a leak, and he stays on guard all day long.**

九五，東鄰殺牛，不如西鄰之禴祭，實受其福。

**Nine in the fifth: The east neighbour slaughters an ox; it does not equal the west neighbour's thin offering, which truly receives the blessing.**

上六，濡其首，厲。

**Six at the top: He wets his head. Danger.**

## Hexagram 64 — 未濟 (Wèi Jì)

### The Judgment

未濟，亨。小狐汔濟，濡其尾，无攸利。

**Wei Ji. Success. A young fox, nearly across, wets his tail. No journey is good.**

初六，濡其尾，吝。

**First six: He wets his tail. Humiliation.**

九二，曳其輪，貞吉。

**Nine in the second: He drags back his wheel. Hold fast: good fortune.**

六三，未濟，征凶，利涉大川。

**Six in the third: Not yet completed. To advance brings misfortune. It is good to cross the great river.**

九四，貞吉，悔亡，震用伐鬼方，三年有賞于大國。

**Nine in the fourth: Hold fast: good fortune. Regret passes. Stirred to action, he attacks the Demon Region; for three years there are rewards from the great kingdom.**

六五，貞吉无悔，君子之光，有孚，吉。

**Six in the fifth: Hold fast: good fortune, no regret. The noble man's light — there is trust. Good fortune.**

上九，有孚于飲酒，无咎。濡其首，有孚，失是。

**Nine at the top: There is trust in drinking wine. No blame. He wets his head; though there is trust, he loses what is right.**
