The I Ching — Tilford Translation
Translator: Robert Tilford
Published edition of 2026-11-22, landed on the shelf the same day by Robert Tilford's ruling.
The core text only: 450 statement units (64 hexagrams — judgments and lines, plus the two yong lines of Hexagrams 1 and 2). The Ten Wings stand outside this edition.
Comparison standard: Legge (1899). Textual consultation and variant-reading cross-checks on flagged passages were assisted by DeepSeek; all translation decisions and final renderings are the translator's. The edition's front matter claims no independent expert review; that review remains an open item on the translator's record.
