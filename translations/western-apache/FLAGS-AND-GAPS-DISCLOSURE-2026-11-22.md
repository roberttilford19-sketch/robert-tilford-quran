   *ígonłgaashíí* "witchcraft/sorcery" (Galatians 5:20), agent noun
   *ídaanłgaashíí* "sorcerer" (Revelation 22:15), and, for the

   the choice of one consistent spelling is the lane's, and it is

   PASS on all 7 checks after application.
