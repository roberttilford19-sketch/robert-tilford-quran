# Western Apache (apw) — Working Glossary for the Quran Lane

Built 2026-11-18, Phase B1. Corpus-led, per the Phase A inventory
(`SOURCE-INVENTORY.md` in this folder).

**Citation key.**
- **NT** = *Bik'ehgo'ihi'nań Biyati'* (Western Apache New Testament,
  Wycliffe Bible Translators / American Bible Society, via eBible.org),
  staged in this lane at `sources/apwnt-verses.json` (7,957 of 7,957
  verses parsed and verified). The NT is a witness and lexicon source
  for word forms; the translation itself is built from the Locked
  English Master, never copied from the NT's renderings.
- **UCLA** = UCLA Phonetics Lab Archive, Western Apache word lists
  (archive.phonetics.ucla.edu, Language/APW), cited by list year and
  entry number. The 1996 lists use a phonetic spelling; the 1982 list
  is nearer the practical orthography.
- **CONSTRUCTION** = a Quran-specific term with no attested precedent,
  built descriptively from attested material and flagged as such.
  Never dressed as a dictionary entry.

**Hard fence kept:** no sister-language padding. Every form below is
attested in Western Apache material. Nothing is borrowed from Navajo,
Mescalero, Chiricahua, or Plains Apache sources.

**Rule A** applies to English-side decisions (divine singular vs.
plural), as in every lane. **Zakat carries no percentage** — the Quran
gives none (Robert's standing rule).

## H. Constructions — flagged, never dressed as dictionary entries

These Quran-specific terms have no attested precedent in the staged
sources. Each is built descriptively from attested material; the
build is shown so a checker can take it apart.

- **C1. zakat** (the obligatory giving) — CONSTRUCTION:
  *Bik'ehgo'ihi'nań begoz'aaníí k'ehgo tédaat'iyéhíí baa nné'íí*,
  "the giving to the poor according to God's decree" — built on
  begoz'aaníí (law, decree; NT Matt 6:33) and the almsgiving idiom
  tédaat'iyéhíí baa nné' (NT Matt 6:2). **No percentage is stated,
  anywhere, ever** — the Quran gives none (Robert's standing rule).
- **C2. sadaqa** (voluntary charity) — CONSTRUCTION:
  *gozhǫ́ǫ́go tédaat'iyéhíí baa nné'íí*, "joyful giving to the poor" —
  built on gozhǫ́ǫ́ (joy, blessedness; NT Luke 23:43) and the
  almsgiving idiom (NT Matt 6:2).
- **C3. mosque** — CONSTRUCTION: *da'okąąh goz'ąą*, the NT's temple
  word ("prayer place," NT Acts 3:1–2), carried over. Flagged: the
  NT word names the Jerusalem temple; its use for a mosque is a lane
  decision, recorded here.
- **C4. the Absolute** (aṣ-Ṣamad, Q 112:2) — CONSTRUCTION:
  *Dahazhį' Golį́į́híí*, "the One who is forever" — built on
  dahazhį' (forever; NT Matt 6:13). The fuller sense of Ṣamad (the
  Eternal Refuge, besought by all) is not carried by any single
  attested word; the construction renders the eternity and flags
  the rest as unrendered.
- **C5. to take refuge** (aʿūdhu) — CONSTRUCTION: *Bik'ehgo'ihi'nań
  bich'į' nánshdáh*, "I come to God" — built on bich'į' (to, toward)
  and nánshdáh (I come; NT Matt 24:37). The shelter nuance of the
  Arabic is approximated, not matched; flagged.
- **C6. guidance** (hudā) — CONSTRUCTION pattern: *intín …
  ch'í'ṉah áiléh*, "to show (someone) the road" — built on itín
  (way; NT John 14:6), niłk'ídezdǫh (straight; NT Matt 3:3), and
  ch'í'ṉah ádndle' (show yourself; NT Matt 8:4).
- **C7. the Day of Resurrection** — CONSTRUCTION:
  *nanezna'íí nádaabihoł'ṉaahíí bijįį*, "the day the dead are
  raised" — built on NT Matt 10:8. (The Day of Judgment itself is
  attested directly; see Section A, judgment.)
- **C8. surah, chapter** — no lexical coinage. The draft sections
  the book by the standard grid address (surah:verse) under the
  word naltsoos (book). Flagged as a formatting decision, not a
  translation.
- **C9. jinn** — rendered *ch'iidn*, the attested spirit word
  (NT Matt 10:8; Acts 2:27), with this flag: ch'iidn in the corpus
  names spirits of the dead and evil spirits; the Quran's jinn are
  a created people. The rendering is a lane decision for the
  checker stage to weigh, recorded here in the open.
- **C10. People of the Book** — CONSTRUCTION: *naltsoos
  bidaayodlaaníí*, "those who believe the Book" — built on
  daayodlaaníí (believers; NT Acts 2:44) and naltsoos (book;
  NT Rev 22:18–19).

## I. The gap list — core Quran vocabulary with no attested form found

Found nowhere in the staged NT corpus or the UCLA lists consulted.

1. **Sorcery, witchcraft** — RESOLVED 2026-11-18 by Robert's
   Ruling 1 (application record: Section J112 below and
   APPLY-RULING-1-2026-11-18.md). The **-łgash family** is
   ADMITTED: practice noun **ígonłgaashíí** ("sorcery,
   witchcraft"); agent noun **ídaanłgaashíí** ("sorcerer(s),
   magician(s)"). Attested in the lane's own NT corpus — three
   dedicated tokens, and no other use of the root anywhere in
   the corpus: ígonłgaashíí at Galatians 5:20 ("witchcraft");
   ínlgashíí at Revelation 9:21 ("sorceries"); ídaanlgashi at
   Revelation 22:15 ("sorcerers"). The Revelation spellings
   (plain l, short vowel) are **variants of record, not used in
   the text**: the book's spelling standard is the Galatians
   pattern (ł, long aa, final íí), and the agent noun is spelled
   ídaanłgaashíí on that pattern. Corroboration: Bray 1998's
   entry "do evil or witchcraft — da'ohni' or da'oi'di'" (a verb
   phrase, dialect variants), as quoted in published reviews of
   the dictionary; its exact spelling and page remain a
   human-pass confirmation. Ruled by Robert Tilford on the
   2026-11-18 live call (Choice A, "Do it"), on the evidence of
   GAP-HUNT-2026-11-18.md. The ruling closed 57 seats (58 inline
   markers replaced, plus the whole-verse seats 2:102 and 113:4
   built); the verb-form seats ride construction C72. NOT
