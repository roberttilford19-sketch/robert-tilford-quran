Status: a DIALECT EDITION of the Locked English Master — the same
translation in South African English spelling and usage. Not a new
translation and not a re-translation: every word is the master's
own, changed only where the recorded conversion list
(DIALECT-CONVERSION-LIST.tsv, in this folder) changes its
spelling: 98 pairs, 388 word-forms across 6,236 verses. South
African usage equals the Australian conversion on the 96 pairs
that lane attested, and the list carries them identically,
disclosed as such; the list goes beyond it at exactly two
attested seats — meager->meagre at 34:16 and
maneuvering->manoeuvring at 8:16. Rule A and every settled ruling
of the master stand untouched in substance; the 6:86 correction
(Elisha) rides as the master carries it. Sajda notes stand at
their seats, as in the master. One conversion is seat-scoped and
disclosed on the list: practice->practise at the 12 verbal seats
only; the 3 nominal seats keep the noun form, as South African
usage requires.
