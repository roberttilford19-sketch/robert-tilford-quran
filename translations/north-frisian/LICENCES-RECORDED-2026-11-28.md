# North Frisian (Mooring) lane — licences and source record (2026-11-28)

- **English Wiktionary via kaikki.org** — North Frisian dump
  (kaikki.org-dictionary-NorthFrisian.jsonl, 1,546 entries /
  1,459 headwords / 16,092 forms), held at
  sources/kaikki-frr.jsonl. Wiktionary material is CC BY-SA.
  Used as attestation (fact and citation), never as copied
  wording. The Mooring dialect screen applied to it is
  implemented in sources/harvest.py and disclosed in
  BUILD-SPEC: 463 entries carry Mooring senses, 113 entries are
  untagged pan-dialect entries, 968 entries attested only for
  other dialects were excluded entirely; derived inventories
  at sources/mooring-forms.json (3,685 forms),
  sources/mooring-plurals.json (268 attested plurals) and
  sources/eng2frr-mooring.json (533 bridge keys, record only).
- **LearnFrisian** — "North Frisian Dictionary: Mooring,
  Söl'ring and Fering Words" (learnfrisian.com; the page states
  every word was checked in the dictionary of friesisch.net and
  in Wiktionary, CC BY-SA 4.0). The Mooring column only (178
  English–Mooring pairs) was harvested by hand from the fetched
  page into sources/learnfrisian-mooring.json and used as
  attestation / corroboration. Its Söl'ring and Fering columns
  were never seated; its West Frisian column served only as the
  page's meaning key.
- **Bendsen (1860)** — Bende Bendsen, *Die nordfriesische
  Sprache nach der Moringer Mundart* (Leiden: E. J. Brill,
  1860; ed. M. de Vries). Public domain by age. Full text held
  at sources/bendsen1860_djvu.txt (archive.org item
  dienordfriesisch00benduoft). Historical control /
  corroboration ONLY: no 1860 spelling was seated as a modern
  form (see BUILD-SPEC, Historical Control Bar).
- **Outzen (1837)** and **Johansen (1862)** — public-domain
  controls named in the candidate brief; not downloaded for
  this build. Johansen is Fering-Öömrang and would in any case
  be barred as a source of Mooring forms.
- **Clemens New Testament (1870)** — Söl'ring; NOT used (a
  different dialect's text; see BUILD-SPEC).
- **Modern print Mooring dictionaries** (Petersen 1973;
  Jörgensen 1978; Sjölin, Walker & Wilts 1988; Basiswörterbuch
  Deutsch-Friesisch, Mooringer Mundart, 2000) — in copyright,
  none held at the desk; named here only as the checker's
  likely resources, usable under dictionary fair use (fact and
  citation) if lawfully held.
- **Locked English Master** — the house master text
  (~/workspace/ayah-audit/corrected/ayah-companion-manuscript.md);
  verse grid held at master-verses.json (6,236 verses).
