# North Frisian (Mooring) lane — glossary of seated key forms

All forms below trace to the harvested Mooring inventory
(sources/mooring-forms.json) as screened by verify.py.

- **Önj** — in. **noome** — name. The Basmalah seats these two
  attested words; **God** and the mercy names stand as GAPs
  (see BUILD-SPEC: Bendsen 1860 attests Godd / Göd in 1860
  spelling, held as historical corroboration only).
- **düüwel** — devil (Mooring-attested; the demonic side of the
  religious vocabulary is attested where the divine side, in
  the held modern inventories, is not — recorded as found).
- **schörk** — church. **bade** — to ask politely, to beg, to
  request; seated for "pray" (the Frisian prayer verb, cognate
  with German *beten*), disclosed here.
- **liiwe** — to believe (also glossed "to think" in the
  sources). **liiwde** — love (noun).
- **bök** — book. **uurd** — word. **stich** — path / road.
  **wäi** — way. **rucht** — right / just / righteous.
- **wråål** — world. **jard** — earth. **siie** — sea / lake
  (homonym in the sources). **däi** — day. **nåcht** — night.
  **laawen** — life (also seated for "alive"). **düüs** — death.
  **hart** — heart. **blödj** — blood.
- **moon** — man / husband. **wüf** — woman / wife.
  **wüset** — woman (LearnFrisian-attested variant, recorded
  here; the dictionary seats wüf). **bjarn** — child.
  **saan** — son. **doochter** — daughter. **taatje** — father.
  **mam** — mother. **brouder** — brother. **söster** — sister
  (LearnFrisian attests the variant süster). **kining** — king.
  **mansche** — person / human.
- **freese** — peace. **krich** — war. **kåmp** — fight / battle.
- Homonyms attested in the sources, disclosed: **win** — wine /
  wind; **müs** — mouse / mouth; **blees** — leaf / page /
  newspaper; **klook** — clock / bell; **moune** — moon / month;
  **san** — sun (and, as a possessive, his / its — the engine
  seats the noun by dictionary key and the pronoun by its own
  key; both trace to Mooring entries).
- Pronouns: **ik** I, **dü** you (sg, seated for all "you"),
  **hi** he (pan-dialect Wiktionary entry), **jü** she,
  **hat** it, **we** we, **jam** you (pl), **ja** they / them;
  objects **me**, **de**, **ham**, **har**, **üs**;
  possessives **man**, **dan**, **san**, **harn**, **üüsen**,
  **jarnge**, **jare**.
- Negation: **ai** — not / no.
- Numbers: **ån** 1, **twäär** 2, **tra** 3, **fjouer** 4,
  **fiiw** 5, **seeks** 6, **soowen** 7, **oocht** 8,
  **nüügen** 9, **tiin** 10, **hunert** 100. "Thousand" has no
  held Mooring seat and stands as a GAP.
