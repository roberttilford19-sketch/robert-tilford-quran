# Il Coran par furlan — the Quran in Friulian

A complete rendering of the Quran in Friulian — all 6,236 verses, translated from the Locked English Master by Robert Tilford. The language base is the official Friulian coinè (La grafie uficiâl, Decree 392 of 25 October 1996), the standard based mainly on Central Friulian; dialect forms are held out, and the base is named on the book's face.

Status: **DRAFT — NOT PUBLISHED.** 59 seats stand open with visible GAP markers where no attested Friulian form was found in the harvested sources; no word was invented to fill them, and nothing was silently borrowed from Italian. No first-translation claim is made — no Friulian Qur'an was found in the catalogues searched, which is recorded as NOT FOUND, not as proof that none exists.
