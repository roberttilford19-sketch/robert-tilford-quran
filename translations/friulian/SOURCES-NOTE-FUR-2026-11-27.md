# Friulian lane — licence verification record
Recorded 2026-11-27 (build opening), per CANDIDATE-BRIEF-FRIULIAN §§2–3
and Robert Tilford's ruling of 2026-11-27 (21:16 CST) adopting the
brief's desk recommendations wholesale. House rule applied throughout:
dictionary and Bible material is used for FACT AND CITATION ONLY, never
the maker's wording, whatever its licence.

## Open / public-domain sources harvested at the desk

- **Jacopo Pirona, *Vocabolario friulano* (1871)** — PUBLIC DOMAIN by
  age. OCR full text retrieved from archive.org item
  `pironavocabolariofriulano` (file Pirona_Vocabolario_friulano_djvu.txt,
  2,652,306 bytes) and held at sources/pirona1871.txt. House use:
  attestation-pointing for the older language; 19th-century spellings
  pre-date the official orthography, so a Pirona-only form is never
  seated in the modern text without corroboration in a modern source
  (the Culòs text, Wiktionary, or the fur.wikipedia corpus).
- **English Wiktionary, Friulian entries** — CC BY-SA. 1,903 lemma
  titles in Category:Friulian_lemmas harvested via the MediaWiki API
  2026-11-27; wikitext held at sources/wikt-pages.json, parsed lexicon
  (lemma → part of speech + English glosses) at
  sources/lexicon-wikt.json. Crowd-sourced: used as attestation with
  corroboration against Pirona, Culòs, or the corpus for load-bearing
  seats, per the brief §2.
- **Friulian Wikipedia (fur.wikipedia.org)** — CC BY-SA. Full
  pages-articles dump retrieved from dumps.wikimedia.org 2026-11-27
  (furwiki-latest-pages-articles-multistream.xml.bz2, 5,276,949 bytes);
  running-text frequency list (1,832,791 tokens) at
  sources/furwiki-freq.txt. House use: attested modern usage and
  spelling in the official orthography — attestation-pointing, never a
  copying base.
- **Ermes Culòs, *Il Vanzeli di Mateo* (Gospel of Matthew, Western
  Friulian)** — © 2007 the translator, hosted as Project Gutenberg
  eBook #27460; text retrieved from the PG mirror (gutenberg.pglaf.org)
  2026-11-27 and held at sources/culos-matteo.txt. Copyright retained
  by the translator: house use is FACT AND CITATION ONLY (which
  Friulian term the text uses, cited by chapter/verse) — never a
  copying base. Western Friulian dialect in the translator's own
  orthography (š/ğ spellings), not the standard coinè: a Culòs-only
  form is seated only in its coinè spelling and flagged.

- **Grant Dizionari Bilengâl Talian–Furlan (GDBTF), ARLeF** — © ARLeF /

  No bulk reliance was made in this build.
