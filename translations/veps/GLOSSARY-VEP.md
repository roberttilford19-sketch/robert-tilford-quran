## H. Flagged constructions (disclosed; attested stems only)
endustai — prophet: agent noun (-i, as in kaivai, kändai,
opendai) of endustada "to foretell, herald" (W).
sananvedäi — messenger: sana "word" + vedäi "bringer, leader"
(both W); regular compound.
ozutuz — revelation (noun): -uz deverbal of ozutada (W),
pattern of toivotuz (W). Verb constructions preferred.
lahjad gollile — alms, zakāh: "gifts for the poor" (lahj W;
gollič D). Descriptive device, not a term.
angel — angel: corpus-observed loan (GiellaLT corpus harvest of
Veps Wikipedia: angel, angeloiden). Dictionary-unattested in the
sources read; every seat logged in batch notes.
Sudindan päiv — the Day of Judgement: sudida (W) + päiv (W),
descriptive.
armahtai — the Merciful: agent of armahtada (W).
antta andaks — to forgive: phrase on antta (W).
