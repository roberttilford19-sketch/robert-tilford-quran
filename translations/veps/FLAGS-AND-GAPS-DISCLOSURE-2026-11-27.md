# Flags and Gaps Disclosure — Veps (vep)

**Status of the book: DRAFT — NOT PUBLISHED.**

The Veps Quran translation in this folder was built attestation-first: every word
form is seated from the harvested sources named in GLOSSARY-VEP.md, or it is not
seated at all. Where no attested Veps form was found, the verse carries a visible
gap marker, ⟦GAP: term⟧, in the text itself. Nothing was invented, and nothing was
silently borrowed from Finnish, Karelian, or Russian.

**Census of the landed build (2026-11-28, mechanically counted and independently
reconciled by the checker): 558 verses carry gaps; 768 gap markers in total; 330
distinct gap terms.** 5,678 of the 6,236 verses are fully rendered. The first
build's census (495 seats / 692 markers) was superseded by the remediation pass;
the full reconciliation — every term added, removed, or re-counted, with its
ground — is in REMEDIATION-LOG-2026-11-28.md, and the gate-by-gate checker record
is in the CHECKER-REPORT files in this folder.

The most frequent gap terms of the landed build: oath (37), jinn (27), throne
(21), curse (17), palm (15), Jews (15), fasting (15), fruit (12), cave (9),
divorce (9), usury (8), dust (7), sorcery (7), sabbath (6), semen (6), blessed
(5), wombs (5), garden (earthly/Eden seats), camel, measure, well, provisions,
descendants, generation. The complete term-by-term census with seat lists is in
the lane record (~/workspace/ayah-veps/work/remediation/gap-census-remediated.md,
regenerated at landing).

**Flagged constructions** (regular derivations on attested stems, disclosed in
GLOSSARY-VEP.md §§H, J, K — not dictionary seats): endustai, sananvedäi, ozutuz,
angel (corpus-observed, dictionary-unattested, disclosed in the attestation
audit), kelʹtand, tirptai/tirptand, heitai, selʹgenzoituz, libutamine, vägest-
and päzu- families, eläbetai, muštelend. **Judgment seats** (logged in the
remediation log): Kitketud renderings of "blessed" where no attested adjective
exists; tatad ("fathers who were before") for forefathers; the throne rendered
by the disclosed Jumalan vald device or GAP, seat by seat.

**Checker history, in the open:** the first build failed the fidelity and token
gates and was not landed. The remediation pass (2,266 verses changed) was then
checked by four successive independent checker passes; the first three found a
bounded residue of capitalized Finnish-shaped forms and each residue was repaired
and re-checked, until the fourth delta pass ruled every gate PASS with the full
sweep clean. The losing findings are preserved in the checker reports in this
folder, not erased.

**Referred, not adjudicated:** the küž- spelling variant and the epäil-,
valehtelij-, ihmeh-, hänelle, and judalai- families were recorded by the checkers
as present but were outside the commissioned gate in every round; they stand
referred for a future adjudication and are named here so the record is complete.

**No native-speaker review** was made or required before shelving (lane condition
C5); the checker is independent of the builders, not of the norm's sources (see
the manuscript's front matter). No claim is made that this is the first Quran
translation into Veps.
