## H. Flagged constructions (allowed, disclosed — the sister
## lanes' Section H pattern)
- il Cudesch — "the Book" as the Qur'an's title device, on
  attested cudesch (PG). Capitalised as a title, disclosed in
  the front matter.
- la via dretga — "the straight path", descriptive on attested
  via + dretg.
- sa terrar / terrada — prostration, descriptive on attested
  terrar (HUN) and terrada (PG, under Niederwerfung).
- The liar device — che di manzegnas ("who speaks lies"), on
  attested dir + manzegna (PG); no agent-noun form is attested
  and none is invented.
- Segner seats "the Lord" (God); signur / patrun seat human
  lord/master (PG). The split is by sense and is logged where
  a seat could be read either way.
- cardientscha is the standing faith seat; cretta (PG) is the
  recorded variant and is not mixed in.
- crair is conjugated on its crai- line (PAR gives crai- and
  cart- doublets; both attested). One line held through the
  book.
- almosna seats zakāh (PG Almosen). Disclosed here and in the
  front matter: it is the attested Romansh alms-word; no
  percentage or rate is ever attached (Rule A).
- The Basmalah: En num da Dieu, il Buntadaivel, il
  Misericordaivel — both adjectives attested (PG), one for
  each of the Master's mercy names.
