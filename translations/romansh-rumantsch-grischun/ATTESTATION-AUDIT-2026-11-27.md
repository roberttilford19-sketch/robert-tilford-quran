# Romansh lane — Qur'anic-term attestation audit
Recorded 2026-11-27, before any seating, per the lane's build
conditions. Edition: Rumantsch Grischun (RG), per Robert's ruling of
2026-11-27 adopting the candidate brief's recommendation.
Sources consulted seat by seat: PG = Pledari Grond Rumantsch
Grischun→German data (entries_RG-DE.txt, 215,614 lines; the Lia
Rumantscha's dictionary, openly licensed — LICENCES A1); PAR = the
Pledari Grond conjugator database (3,817 verbs, full paradigms);
HUN = the PG-derived Rumantsch Grischun Hunspell form inventory
(160,802 forms, MIT — LICENCES A2); K = English Wiktionary via
kaikki.org (POINTER only — no seat rests on K alone); W = Romansh
Wikipedia running text (frequency corroboration only). Bible control
(citation grade): the Bibla da Cuera (1718/1719) and the BFBS Bible
of 1870, both Sursilvan, public domain — BIBLE-CONTROL-VERIFICATION-
2026-11-27.md. No seat below rests on a Bible text; the control
confirms the religious register exists in written Romansh.

## Grammar anchors (all forms verified against PAR/HUN)
- esser: sun, es, è, essan, essas, èn; impf era…; subj saja…;
  pp stà; gerund essend. avair: hai, has, ha, avain, avais, han;
  impf aveva…; subj haja…; pp gì. Future is periphrastic for every
  verb: vegnir a + infinitive (PAR, all 3,817 verbs).
- Negation: na … betg around the verb (na omissible in modern RG
  usage; both patterns attested — Wiktionary betg entry, RG).
- Personal names and scripture titles are carried in the Master's
  forms (Moses, Jesus, Mary, Abraham, Noah, the Torah, the Gospel,
  the Psalms), unaltered — disclosed in the front matter, the
  sister lanes' name policy.
