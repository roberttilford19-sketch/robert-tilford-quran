### D1. мерсь ×161 against the spec's ёвтась preference

Recomputed: мерсь occurs 161 times; ёвтась 310 times. BUILD-SPEC
prints мерсь inside its own past-tense paradigm ("3sg -сь
(тейсь, содась, мерсь→…") and then hedges: "use мерсь only if
sure; safer 3sg past of меремс is ёвтась", with ёвтась named
the standing narrative form. мерсь is the paradigm-regular 3sg
past of the attested lemma меремс — not an invented form — but
the builders used the hedged form 161 times against the spec's
stated preference, undisclosed in the batch notes. DISCLOSED,
not regularised: the seats stand as built.

### D3. Capitalisation of divine epithets and pronouns

Beyond the BUILD-SPEC's letter ("sentence-initial and proper
names only, plus Паз and its pronouns where the Master
capitalises"), the book capitalises divine pronouns and
epithets mid-sentence throughout: the checker recomputed Минь
("We" of God) ×1,386 mid-sentence and Сон ×831, and participles
such as Содыця and Простиця stand capitalised as divine titles;
Мон/Тон are capitalised in some direct-speech seats. The
practice is consistent and reverential in intent. DISCLOSED,
not regularised.

### D4. Latin vocative "O" ×40 against Erzya vocative practice

Recomputed: the Latin letter "O" stands as the Master's
vocative before names in 40 seats (e.g. 18:86, 18:94, 19:7,
19:12, 19:27), carried with the names under the names policy.
Erzya vocative practice exists elsewhere in the same book —
Cyrillic "Эй," at 2:61 and 2:275 and Cyrillic "О" at
2:189-type seats — so the practice is inconsistent between the
Latin carry and the Erzya vocative. DISCLOSED, not regularised.
