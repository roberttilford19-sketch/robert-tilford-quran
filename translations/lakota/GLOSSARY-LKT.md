*A candidate first complete Lakota Quran draft — no complete version found after research, pending bibliographic and community confirmation. Every entry needs native-speaker review before any community use.*
*Quran-alone rule: terms are defined by the Quran itself — no hadith, no tafsir, no traditional specifics.*

## §1. Divine names (transliterated, never translated)

| Lakota (draft) | English (Tilford) | Arabic | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Allah | God | الله | Always transliterated. NEVER Tȟuŋkášila / Wakȟáŋ Tȟáŋka or any traditional Lakota divine name. |
| Ar-Rahman | the Beneficent | الرحمن | Always transliterated. |
| Ar-Rahim | the Merciful | الرحيم | Always transliterated. |
| Rabb | Lord | رب | Always transliterated when the divine title. Possessives: `Rabb mitáwa` (my Lord), `Rabb nitáwa` (your Lord), `Rabb uŋtáwa` (our Lord) — forms to be normalized. |
| Rabb al-Alamin | Lord of the Worlds | رب العالمين | Always transliterated. |

**Basmalah** (identical in all 112 openings): `*Allah čhažé ektá, Ar-Rahman, Ar-Rahim.*`
("In the name of Allah, Ar-Rahman, Ar-Rahim." — normalized from worker variants;
see FLAGS-LKT.md for the rejected variants and the native-review note.)
