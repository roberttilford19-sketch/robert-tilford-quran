History: On 2026-10-05 Robert Tilford sent a notice-first letter to the
Standing Rock Sioux Tribe's language office (the Iyapi Director),
announcing this scaffold before any publication and undertaking that, if
no reply arrived by 2027-01-03, it would stay unpublished, and that any
objection from that office or from community members would end the matter
permanently. The only reply ever received was an auto-reply stating the
addressee had left her post; no human response ever arrived. On
2026-11-14 Robert Tilford ruled the letter's silence-date term superseded,
on the ground that the invitation was sent in good faith and the chair
stood vacant, and ordered the scaffold shelved as availability only.

Ruling — 2026-11-18: Robert Tilford ended the review-gate regime and
lifted the availability and withdrawal undertakings that stood in this
manifest. The scaffold stands on the shelf as what it is — an unfinished
scaffold — so that anyone who comes looking may find it. No outreach has
been made to any tribe about it.
