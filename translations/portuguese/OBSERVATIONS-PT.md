## 2. Terminology decisions for the native reviewer

- **God = Deus throughout; body "Allah"/"Alá" count = 0** (verified). "Muhammad" kept as in the English everywhere (8 occurrences incl. the surah-47 title); "Maomé" was not introduced without a ruling — same decision point the Italian run flagged at 33:40, here applied consistently rather than mixed.
- **"The Reminder" = "a Lembrança"** fixed by the brief and applied at 41:41 and 43:5 (the Urdu two-renderings weak spot did not recur).
- **Divine epithets beyond the brief's fixed pair** still vary by design of mirroring the English: "the Compassionate" → "o Compassivo" (55:1, 59:22, 67:20, 67:29); "the Most Merciful" → mostly "o Mais Misericordioso" (21×) but "o Misericordiosíssimo" at 78:37–38. A reviewer should decide whether to collapse these to one form.
- **jinn** is rendered two ways across slices: "gênios" (14×) and kept "jinn" (14×). Needs one project-wide ruling.
- **People-name forms are mixed by necessity:** established Brazilian forms used where they exist (Tamud 12×, Faraó, Ló, Sabá), while "Thamood" (12×), "Tamude" (2×), "Hud" and "Shuaib"/"Saleh" remain in or near the English spellings in other slices. A pt-BR norming pass should unify (suggested: Tamud, Hude/Suaibe only if the reviewer confirms them as established).
- **zakat/almsgiving:** "caridade obrigatória" (8×) for the obligatory charity and "caridade"/"esmola(s)" elsewhere — **no percentage anywhere** (verified 0 "%" signs), per the Quran-alone rule.
- Fixed glossary otherwise held: Senhor, Mensageiro/profeta, o Livro, o Alcorão, a Escritura, a Vida Futura, o Jardim, o Paraíso, o Fogo/o Inferno, oração, jejum, "os que creem / os que não creem", injustos, hipócritas, sinais, Dia do Juízo / Dia da Ressurreição. Second person is você (3,751×) / vocês (2,812×) consistently; no tu/você mixing.

## 3. Literal renderings — known weak spots for a native reader

- **81:4** — "When the relationships are suspended" rendered literally: "Quando os relacionamentos forem suspensos" (flagged; reviewer may want an idiomatic alternative).
- **83:9, 83:20** — "A numerical book" rendered literally "Um livro numerado" (likely "um registro escrito").
- **Kept-literal items flagged by translators:** 41:10 "estabilizadores" (likely mountains), 42:7 "a Cidade Central", 44:43 "A Árvore da Amargura", 68:42 "a Canela", 68:16 "focinho", 68:48 "o Companheiro do Peixe", 26:224 "em todo estilo", 24:31 "o que suas mãos direitas possuem".
- **Transliterations kept as in the English** pending a norming pass: Sijjeen, Elliyyeen/Elliyyoon (source spelling itself varies), Quraish, at-Tariq, Saqar, Zanjabeel, Salsabeel, Tuwa, Russ (50:12), and the idol names of 71:23.
- **93:3** — body rendered as written ("nem Ele se esqueceu") although the surah intro implies "displeased"; source-level inconsistency, reviewer's call.
- Register variance across the 12 translators is expected; long legal/household verses (e.g., 4:11–12, 5:110, 33:50, 33:53) mirror the English's dense single sentences and read heavily. Sensitive passages (4:34, 24:2–9, 24:31–33) were translated plainly and literally — a Brazilian reviewer should check tone and legal naturalness.

## 4. Structure and discipline

- Format identical to the Italian build: Portuguese title block (MANUSCRITO PORTUGUÊS — RASCUNHO DE REVISÃO, não publicação), Índice, Parte I · A Tradução, 114 sections with the Arabic surah name, an italic intro line, basmalah Arabic + italic fixed-formula line "Em nome de Deus, o Beneficente, o Misericordioso." in the 112 surahs that carry the frame (surah 1 counts it as 1:1; surah 9 has none), the translator's note inside 5:6 with its continuation after it ("Algumas leituras continuam a passar as mãos até os pés; a leitura impressa aqui diz lavar."), and 15 prostration markers (11 *[prostração — recomendada]*, 4 *[prostração — obrigatória]*) after the verse's Arabic line.
- Quran-alone discipline: no hadith/tafsir material imported, no traditional specifics added, no percentage for zakat.
- The audit is **structural** (counts, sequences, the Arabic character-for-character, markers, residue scan), as for the other drafts of this project: it is not a verse-by-verse philological proof. Final language quality requires a native Brazilian Portuguese reviewer.
