# De Quran óf Pälzisch — the Quran in Pfälzisch

A complete rendering of the Quran in Pfälzisch (Palatine), the ancestral tongue of Victoria/Herzog and Catherine in Ellis County, Kansas — all 6,236 verses with the Arabic alongside, translated from the Locked English Master by Robert Tilford.

**This book is not Volga German** and is never described as such; it stands under its own true name. Its control is Dieter Zimmer's complete Westpfälzisch New Testament (2021), named in the book's front matter with its two limits: New Testament only, West Palatine in centre.
