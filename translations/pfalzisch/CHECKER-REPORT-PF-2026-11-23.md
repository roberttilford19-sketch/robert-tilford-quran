## 2. Fidelity spot-check — 10 verses against the master, word by word

1. **2:255** — Substantially complete; no doctrine imported. Two defects: (i) the slumber/sleep distinction is collapsed — "slumber" is held as **[Schlof]** and "nor sleep" is rendered *kä Müdichkät* ("nor tiredness"), a substitution, not a translation of "sleep"; (ii) "the Sustainer" → *de Erhalder* ("the Maintainer") is an acceptable periphrasis. Throne, intercession, knowledge and preservation clauses all present.
2. **5:6 (with translator's note)** — **The note is present, in place, in brackets and italics, in the same position as the master:** *\*[Manchi Lesunge mache es Wische bis se de Fieß weiter; die Lesung, wo dò gedruckt és, saht wasche.]\** — this seat **passes**. Defects in the verse body: the **[toilet]** gap stands mid-verse, and "you had contact with women" is narrowed to *ner Fraue beriehrt hann* ("you touched women") with the drift form *Fraue* (§3).
3. **24:35** — Complete: niche, lamp, glass, planet (the master's word, correctly followed over "star"), olive tree, oil, Light upon Light all present. One addition: "God is cognizant of everything" → *Gott wääß alles **genau*** — "exactly" is added; minor. "God thus cites the parables" → *fiehrt … die Gleichnisse … aa* (*aanfiehre*) is a defensible periphrasis.
4. **55:1 — FAIL at this seat.** Master: "The Compassionate" — this is ar-Rahman, the surah's own name. The manuscript prints **De Barmherziche**, which the brief's locked table assigns to ar-Rahim. The locked pair is *de Gnädiche* (ar-Rahman) / *de Barmherziche* (ar-Rahim), and 1:3 prints both correctly side by side. Required correction in §5.
5. **1:5** — Faithful. "It is You we worship, and upon You we call for help" → *Dich allän bäde mir aan, un bei Dir allän rufe mir óm Hilf.* No addition, no omission. (The pronoun form *Dir* is a §3 drift item, not a fidelity item.)
6. **2:282** (longest legal verse) — Complete in substance, including the repayment-time clause and the spot-transaction exception; nothing dropped from a very long verse, which is to the builders' credit. Two softenings/coinages: "in all fairness" → *én aller **Faierhät*** (unattested coinage, twice in the verse) and the legal category "mentally deficient" → *geistich schwach* ("mentally weak") — a softening of a operative legal term.
7. **4:34** — **No softening.** "then strike them" is rendered plainly (*dann schlaa se*); "protectors and maintainers," the spending ground, the admonish/abandon/strike sequence and "seek no way against them" are all present. "Sublime, Great" → *erhab un groß* is adequate.
8. **9:5** — Complete and unsoftened: kill/capture/besiege/ambush, the repentance exception, prayer and alms (*die Almose*, the locked form) all present. The closing superlatives are pattern-formed (*de Vergebendschde, de Barmherzichschde*) and disclosed by type.
9. **3:7** — One interpretive move to disclose: the master's "seeking descent" is rendered *Zwietracht suche* ("seeking discord") — the evident sense, but not the master's letter. The manuscript also resolves the master's ambiguous punctuation by closing the sentence at "except God." and starting "And those firmly rooted in knowledge say…" — the standard reading, but it is a resolution, not a neutral copy. No content omitted.
10. **7:54** — One substitution: "Blessed is God, **Lord of all beings**" → *de Herr vón alle **Welde*** ("Lord of all **worlds**") — beings replaced by worlds, against the master's distinct wording (and against the locked *Herr vón de Welde* being reserved for "Lord of the Worlds"). "established Himself on the Throne" → *sich óf de Thron geschtellt* ("stood himself on the Throne") is a slight shift. Night/day, sun/moon/stars and creation/command clauses complete.

**Fidelity summary:** no imported doctrine found in any of the 10; no zakat percentage or other Quran-alone violation; one locked-term error (55:1), one substitution (7:54), one legal softening (2:282), one collapsed distinction (2:255), one minor addition (24:35).
