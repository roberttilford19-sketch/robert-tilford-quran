## PIN 1 — THE DICTIONARY: the Pfälzisches Wörterbuch at the Trierer Wörterbuchnetz

**Grade: PARTIAL — complete A–Z in a search interface; no bulk road.**

### What the work is (from the Wörterbuchnetz's own pages and the print record)

- *Pfälzisches Wörterbuch.* Begründet von **Ernst Christmann** (1885–1974), fortgeführt von **Julius Krämer**, bearbeitet von **Rudolf Post** unter Mitarbeit von **Josef Schwing** und **Sigrid Bingenheimer**. Akademie der Wissenschaften und der Literatur, Mainz; Franz Steiner Verlag, Wiesbaden/Stuttgart.
- **Six volumes, not seven** — the memo's "7-volume" is wrong. Print run: Bd. 1 (A–C) 1968; Bd. 2 (D–F) 1975; Bd. 3 (G–J) 1980; Bd. 4 (K–M) 1986; Bd. 5 (L–Schw) 1993; Bd. 6 (Se–Z) 1997; closed with a Beiheft (supplement) 1998. (Trier's own citation line: "6 Bände. Wiesbaden/Stuttgart 1965–1997" — 1965 is the first fascicle; HathiTrust records the set as issued in parts 1965–1998, 6 vols. + supplement.)
- It documents the Palatine dialect vocabulary of the first half of the 20th century, including — by its own scope — the Pfälzisch settlement dialects abroad, Pennsylvania named among them. Material base: a slip archive of roughly 1.5 million slips.

### Coverage across the alphabet — verified

The Wörterbuchnetz presents and cites the complete six-volume work, and live lemma records are independently attested across the alphabet by sources quoting their Wörterbuchnetz lemma addresses verbatim:

- **A:** „auwei, Interj." — woerterbuchnetz.de/PfWB?lemid=A04217 (cited in the BBAW's MEGA digital apparatus).
- **F:** lemid=PF03841 (Wiktionary's Wörterbuchnetz reference module, PfWB examples).
- **L:** „Loos" — PfWB/?lemid=PL02246 (quoted with entry text in a genealogy forum answer sourced to the Wörterbuchnetz).
- **M:** „Milterer → Mülterer" — PfWB?lemid=M03211; „mürbe, Adj." — PfWB?lemid=M03311 (Institut für Geschichtliche Landeskunde citation); „Munken, Munkel, Münkel" — PfWB/?lemid=PM03278, its full entry text quoted verbatim in a public answer.
- **N:** PfWB?lemid=N00940 (cited in a University of Freiburg dissertation's source list).
- **U:** ?lemma=Uebel (Wiktionary reference module, PfWB examples).
- **Z range:** no single Z lemma citation was captured; coverage of the end of the alphabet rests on the complete-set evidence — print Bd. 6 is Se–Z (1997), the supplement followed in 1998, and Trier offers the work as the finished six-volume dictionary, not a partial digitisation. Stated plainly so the gap in the spot-check is on the record.

### The exact nature of the access

- **A search interface.** Lemmas are retrieved one at a time through the Wörterbuchnetz search, each under a stable lemma address (…/PfWB?lemid=…). There is **no bulk file**: no PDF, XML, or text dump of the dictionary is offered for download anywhere on the site.
- The site's navigation carries an **"API Schnittstellen"** (API interfaces) section, and the Trier Center for Digital Humanities has presented the Wörterbuchnetz's "offene Schnittstellen" (open interfaces) publicly (DiÖ workshop, 2021) — so a programmatic road exists in principle. Its PfWB terms and completeness were **not** verified in this pass; no public bulk endpoint for the PfWB dataset was found.
- The only other digital copy of the print work, at HathiTrust, is **"Limited (search only)"** — snippet search behind a rights wall, not full text. Google Books is a limited preview. No page scans stand open on archive.org.
- **Grade reasoning:** the house gate says "bulk-open." A complete dictionary behind a one-lemma-at-a-time interface is the SEAlang shape, which the house graded a supplement, not a bulk source. PfWB is stronger than SEAlang in one respect — it is the whole A–Z of the one authoritative dictionary, where SEAlang's Chavacano was also dialect-off-centre — but on access shape it is the same: **interface-only. PARTIAL.**

## PIN 2 — THE CONTROL TEXT: Palatine scripture standing open

**Grade: PASS — with the control's exact identity and limits stated below.** The memo's "no complete open-access digital control text confirmed" is **overturned**: one stands open in full text, and the memo appears not to have found it.

### The control that exists: Zimmer's West Palatine New Testament

- **Title:** *Es Neie Teschdament — Es lewenniche Wort (ELW).* In full: "Rheinfränggisch – Oschtsaarlännisch – Weschtpälzisch – Oschderdal" (Rhine Franconian – East Saarland – West Palatine – Easter Valley).
- **Translator:** **Dieter Zimmer**, with Andreas Schorr collaborating. Self-published (Selbstverlag, Wilnsdorf). Text status ("Textstand") **May 2021**. Translated from the Greek (Trinitarian Bible Society text and Nestle–Aland 28).
- **Completeness: the whole New Testament.** Verified in the document itself: Matthew opens at p. 9; Revelation runs pp. 811–861; a chronology appendix and a subject-and-word glossary (pp. ~863–876+) close the volume. All 27 books, in canonical order.
- **Access: full text open.** The complete PDF stands on the website of the Evangelische Kirche der Pfalz (the Pfälzer Bibelverein's pages, evpfalz.de), free to open and download, no login, no lending wall: `evpfalz.de/…/Westpfaelzer_Bibel/Neues_Testament_westpfaelzisch_2021_05.pdf`.
- **Dialect centre — the honest limit:** this is **Westpfälzisch** (the Kaiserslautern / Westpfalz variety, shading to East Saarland), one named sub-variety of Pfälzisch — not the Vorderpfälzisch of Speyer and the Rhine plain, and not a pan-Palatine standard (none exists). A build controlled by Zimmer would be seated in West Palatine forms and must say so on its face, exactly as the Chavacano build had to name its centre.
- **Copyright position:** the imprint reads "© Copyright by Dieter Zimmer" — rights held by the living translator, all rights reserved, **no open licence**. The church hosts the PDF with his imprint, so the text is openly readable and downloadable by the rights-holder's own arrangement, but it is not public domain and not openly licensed. As a *control* — read for name forms, orthography, and terminology, never republished — that is the same posture the house accepted for the Chavacano lexicon (openly posted by its author, "all rights reserved," graded usable with the caveat recorded). It may not be copied into any shelf product.
- **No Old Testament by Zimmer was found.** Searches for a Zimmer OT or Psalms returned nothing; the control is NT-only and must be named as such wherever it is used — the same disclosure the Chavacano lane carries.

## BOTTOM LINE

**The gate passes in part — and the part that fails is the dictionary's shape, not the control.**

- **Pin 2 (control): PASS.** If the build were authorised, the control would be exactly this: **Dieter Zimmer's *Es Neie Teschdament — Es lewenniche Wort* (2021 text), the complete New Testament in Westpfälzisch, open in full text on the Pfalz church's own site** — NT-only, West-Palatine-centred, © the translator, used as a control and never reproduced. Every record of the lane would carry those four facts.
- **Pin 1 (dictionary): PARTIAL.** The Pfälzisches Wörterbuch is complete and authoritative and fully searchable A–Z at the Wörterbuchnetz — but interface-only, with no bulk file, the same access shape the house has previously graded as a supplement rather than a bulk source. Two roads could lift it: (a) verification of the Trier API's PfWB coverage and terms, if programmatic lemma harvest is permitted; or (b) a data supply from the Trier Center for Digital Humanities on application, as research centres commonly arrange. Neither was tested in this pass; both are named so the shortfall is exact.
- **Corrections to the memo, for the record:** the dictionary is **six** volumes (1965/68–1997, supplement 1998), not seven; and its second pin's caution — "no complete open-access digital control text confirmed" — is **overturned** by the Zimmer NT standing open at evpfalz.de.
- **What this means for the decision before Robert:** a Pfälzisch build is *not* blocked on sources the way Volga German is. It would rest on a complete NT control (West Palatine) and a complete dictionary behind an interface — a buildable lane under a disclosed PARTIAL on the dictionary limb, if Robert rules the interface-plus-API road sufficient, or a held lane pending the Trier data question, if he does not. That judgement is his; the pins themselves are now desk-verified and stand as graded above.
