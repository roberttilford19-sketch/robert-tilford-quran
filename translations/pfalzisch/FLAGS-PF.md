## Attestation caveat

The 83.63% attestation figure is a floor, not a measurement of the language: the lexicon was machine-tokenised from a pdftotext extraction of the control and splits some of Zimmer's words at line-break artefacts, so forms verified present by direct search (*wisse*, *gudd*, *Nòricht*, *Goddes*, *Fraae*) still score unattested in the token count. The largest remaining unattested classes are the control's own attested minority forms, pattern forms on Zimmer's visible patterns, and carried names (Quran 73×).
