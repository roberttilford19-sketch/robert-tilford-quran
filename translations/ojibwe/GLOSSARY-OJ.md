# GLOSSARY — Ojibwe (Anishinaabemowin) Quran Translation (OJ)

Consolidated 2026-11-22 from the WORKER-BRIEF locked table, the flags files' TERMS sections (all ranges, including the desk's surah 4 flags), at assembly of ayah-companion-manuscript-OJ.md. All forms are provisional and NOT speaker-reviewed. Where workers used more than one form for one English term, every form is listed — the variation is disclosed, not hidden.

## Locked terminology (WORKER-BRIEF — use these exact forms everywhere)

- **God** → Allah
- **Lord** → Ogimaa (provisional)
- **the Beneficent (ar-Rahman)** → Ar-Rahman
- **the Merciful (ar-Rahim)** → Ar-Rahim
- **Quran** → Quran
- **Scripture / the Book** → mazina'igan
- **Messenger** → Rasuul
- **prophet** → Nabii
- **angels** → Aanjel
- **Satan** → Matchi-manidoo
- **jinn** → Jinn
- **Fire / Hell** → ishkote
- **Say, "..."** → Ikido: "..."
- **charity / alms / obligatory charity** → miigiwewin (never a percentage)
- **Basmalah (all 112 openings, byte-identical)** → *Izhinikaazowining: Allah, Ar-Rahman, Ar-Rahim.*

## Known cross-worker variations (disclosed for the independent checker)

- believers: debwe'endamojig (surahs 4–5 road) alongside debwetawijig (surah 3 road) and further per-range forms in the table above.
- disbelievers: gaa-wiin-debwe'endaamosig alongside bawaadamaagejig and further per-range forms in the table above.
- Garden / Paradise: Mino-ayaawin alongside Minwashining and further per-range forms in the table above.
- Conjunct plural endings vary between -oowaad and -aawaad in a small set of high-frequency verbs (see PURPLE-SWEEP-OJ-2026-11-22.md).
- Prostration-note phrasing is not uniform across ranges (15 notes, ~13 distinct phrasings; inventoried in the Purple sweep).

Caveat carried from the census: the Bible control text is the northwestern Ontario (Saulteaux) variety of Ojibwe, while The Ojibwe People's Dictionary is Minnesota Ojibwe; both are double-vowel. Term choices may carry either variety's colouring and are NOT speaker-reviewed.
