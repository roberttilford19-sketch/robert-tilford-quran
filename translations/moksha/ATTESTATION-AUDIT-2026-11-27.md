Recorded 2026-11-27, before any seating, per CANDIDATE-BRIEF-MOKSHA
§8 condition 4. Sources consulted seat by seat: the GiellaLT
giella-mdf stem XML (LGPL v3; harvested at sources/giella/ — cited
"Giella", with the Finnish or French gloss that carries the seat);
the kaikki.org / English Wiktionary Moksha harvest (CC BY-SA; at
sources/kaikki-mdf.jsonl — cited "Wikt"). The full parsed lemma

here and in the glossary. Erzya forms were never consulted and
never seated; Erzya–Moksha MT output was never used (brief §3 bar).

## STAYS GAP — no attested Moksha form; held open, never invented
- **jinn** — no attested Moksha term in the harvest. Сатана is a
  Satan seat and is never substituted silently for jinn; jinn
  seats take ⟦GAP: jinn⟧.
- **pilgrimage (ḥajj)** — no attested Moksha term for the rite.
  Where a verse's sense can be carried descriptively on attested
  words alone (travel to God's house), the descriptive rendering
  is permitted and logged; where the term itself is required, the
  seat takes ⟦GAP: pilgrimage⟧.
- **Sabbath (the institution)** — no attested term for the
  institution was found. Term seats take ⟦GAP: Sabbath⟧;
  descriptive "rest day" phrasing on attested stems is permitted
  and logged.
- **usury** — no attested single term found; seats take
  ⟦GAP: usury⟧ unless the verse's sense is carried by attested
  money/lending words descriptively, logged.
