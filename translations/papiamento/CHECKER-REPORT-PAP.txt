## 3. Orthography spot check — Aruba-etymological leakage

The headline Aruba forms are **absent from the verse text**: *cielo* 0 (vs *shelu* 309), *educacion* 0 in verses (*edukashon* present), *gracia* 0 in verses (vs *grasia* 47), *cas/casa* 0 in verses (vs *kas* 73). The c-before-a/o/u scan over all 6,236 verse lines returns only legitimate *ch-* words (*chikí, cherchi, chansa*…), *cerca* (below), and one loan *completo* (56:65) — there is no broad c-leakage.

**One systematic leakage was found, and it is the report's main correction:**

- **`cerca` for Curaçao `serka` — 84 tokens in 75 distinct verse seats.** The variants file is explicit: var-0084, Curaçao `serka` ↔ Aruba `cerca`. The Bible control is unanimous: `serka` 972 occurrences, `cerca` **0**. The manuscript itself uses the correct `serka` 397 times (398 seats), so this is an inconsistency inside the book, not a chosen norm. First seats: 2:28, 2:37, 2:38, 2:46, 2:54, 2:62, 2:85, 2:87, 2:89, 2:92, 2:94, 2:101, 2:110, 2:112, 2:118, 2:120, 2:145, 2:156, 2:187, 2:203, 2:209, 2:210, 2:213, 2:214, 2:217, 2:245, 2:253, **2:255**, 2:260, 2:262, 2:277, 2:282, 3:14, 3:15, 3:19, 3:37, 3:49, 3:50, 3:55, 3:61 (full seat list reproducible by `grep` on the word — the checker counts, not a sample: 75 seats, 84 tokens).
- Checked and cleared: **`intenshoná`** (from lemma `intencion`, pap-008300) — 34 tokens in 31 seats (first: 2:26, 2:225, 4:26–28, 4:62, 5:3, 5:11…). A checker grep for an *s*-spelled variant (`intensioná`) returns **0**; the *sh* spelling is uniform book-wide and conforms to the declared norm. No fault.

## 4. Fidelity spots — 6 verses against the English slices

Compared against `work/en/surah_NNN.txt` (the Locked English Master slices):

1. **2:255 — faithful.** Every clause of the master is present, in order, with no addition or softening. Only faults are orthographic (*cerca Dje* → *serka Dje*, §3) and the heavy calque *ku eksepshon di / ku eksepshon ku* ×3 for "except" — grammatical and Bible-attested as a phrase (*eksepshon* occurs 28× in the control), so noted, not failed.
2. **5:6 — faithful, one word fails attestation.** The ablution sequence, the ¶ variant note, and the full second half of the verse (impurity, illness/travel, tayammum, "God does not intend to burden you…") are all present and accurate. Sole fault: *elboog* (§1).
3. **24:59 — meaning drift (softening by collapse).** Master: "God is **Knowledgeable and Wise**." Manuscript: "Dios ta **Sabio i Sabio**." Two distinct epithets are collapsed into one word repeated; the "Knowledgeable" half of the meaning is lost. Required fix in §5.
4. **40:67 — faithful.** Dust → seed → embryo → infant → maturity → old age, both purpose clauses, and the "some die sooner" parenthesis all present. *Simia* = "seed" is attested and correct (it does not mean "monkey" here). Only the (b)-grade loans *embrion/beibi* mark the verse.
5. **55:1 — ruling-conformant, master-divergent by design.** Master slice reads "The Compassionate." The manuscript reads "E Klemente.", applying the locked ar-Rahman ruling and matching the surah title. This is the declared ruling overriding the slice's wording — recorded here so the divergence is on the record, not silent.
6. **3:7 — faithful to the master, including the master's own suspect word.** Structure, the foundation/unspecific distinction, and both "except" clauses are preserved, with no additions. The drift risk sits in the master ("seeking descent"), which the Papiamentu mirrors as *desenso* (§1); if the master is corrected to "dissension," this seat must be re-rendered with it.

No additions, omissions, or softenings were found in the other five spots.

## 5. Verdict and required fixes

**PASS WITH CORRECTIONS.** The manuscript is complete (6,236/6,236 verses, 114/114 surahs), every locked ruling tested holds exactly (112 italic Basmalahs + 1:1 + none at Surah 9; 0 "Allah"; 0 "%"; prostration notes 11 + 4; 5:6 ¶ note present), the language sample grades 38 (a) / 10 (b) / 0 (c), and fidelity is clean in 5 of 6 spots. It does not earn an unqualified PASS because the declared norm — "where the Bible's spelling differs, the Bible's spelling wins" — is breached systematically at one word, and one verse loses half an epithet pair.

**Required fixes (precise):**

1. **Global, 75 seats / 84 tokens — `cerca` → `serka`.** Current text (pattern): `… cerca Dje …` / `… a bini cerca …`. Required text: the same sentences with `serka` in every place `cerca` now stands as the preposition "with / near / to". Seats include 2:255 ("intersedé cerca Dje" → "intersedé serka Dje") and the 74 further seats listed in §3. This is a mechanical, meaning-preserving correction; after it, `cerca` must count 0 in verse text.
2. **5:6 — `elboog`.** Current: "laba boso kara i boso man i brasa te na e **elboog**". Required: replace `elboog` with an attested Papiamentu form for "elbow" — none exists in the sources held, so the builder must either attest one from the control/wordlists and record the attestation, or flag the seat in the lane's flags file as the brief requires; the raw Dutch form may not stand unflagged.
3. **24:59 — epithet collapse.** Current: "Dios ta Sabio i Sabio." Required: "Dios ta yen di konosementu i ta Sabio." (restores the master's two epithets, Knowledgeable and Wise, in attested words).
4. **`buig` seats (16: 7:11, 7:12, 7:206, 9:112, 12:4, 13:15, 15:30, 15:31, 15:32, 15:33, 15:98, 16:48, 16:49, 17:61, 18:50, 20:116).** Current pattern: "ta buig". Required pattern: use the manuscript's own adapted prostration verb, as at 55:6 "ta prosterná nan mes" — e.g. 16:49 current "Pa Dios ta buig tur kos den e shelunan…" → required "Pa Dios ta prosterná tur kos den e shelunan…", and likewise at the other 15 seats, preserving each seat's word order otherwise.

**Recommended, not required:** flag or replace the remaining (b)-grade unattested words in §1 (3:7 *desenso/enraisá/spesífiko*, 11:27, 14:50, 28:48, 33:67, 40:67, 45:27) through the lane's flags file, as the worker brief requires flags for unattested choices; disclose in the build record that the Bible control held is OT-only (22 books, no NT); and note the *Klemente* double duty (ar-Rahman and the master's "Clement" for Ḥalīm) in the build record.

*End of checker report. The manuscript was not edited.*
