# Glossary — Papiamento (PAP)

The locked terminology of the Papiamento Ayah Companion, with its ground. "Control" = Beibel na Papiamentu Koriente (2013), held in the lane's sources; "Wordlist" = the Aruba official wordlist dataset (Departamento di Enseñansa Aruba, 2009). Where the wordlist's Aruba etymological spelling differs from the control's Curaçao/Bonaire spelling, the control's spelling is used in the text and the Aruba form is noted here.
