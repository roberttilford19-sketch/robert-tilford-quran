# Meadow Mari lane — attestation audit (2026-11-28)

Method: every seat below was checked seat by seat against the two
inventories harvested at the desk on 2026-11-28 — GiellaLT
giella-mhr stem XML (50,921 lemmas, sources/giella-lemmas-full.json)
[G] and English Wiktionary via kaikki.org (1,220 headwords,
sources/kaikki-mhr-parsed.json) [W]. Combined inventory: 51,416
lemmas (sources/mhr-lemmas.json). A seat is listed only where the
lemma stands in [G] and/or [W] with the gloss shown. Dictionary use
is fact and citation only, never the maker's wording (house law).

## The Hill Mari fence check

the mhr inventories only. Hill Mari (mrj), Northwestern Mari,
Erzya (myv), Udmurt (udm), and Moksha (mdf) inventories were
