## Flags record — both counting conventions stated honestly

Flagged verse references stand at approximately 2,700 across the
17 canonical flags files in the lane's work/ folder. The two
counts on the record differ by counting convention, not by any
difference in the text: the builder counted 2,709 distinct verse
references flagged across the seventeen files (counted per file as
distinct N:M references in flag entries, including 48 in the new
flags_08 — 34 verse flags plus name/term entries); the independent
checker, recounting mechanically, found 2,725 by entry-leading
references summed per file, and 3,001 counting all references
anywhere in the 17 files. The difference is a counting-convention
matter — which reference mentions count as flag entries — and the
order of magnitude and the per-file pattern agree. Both figures
are carried here rather than either being adopted silently. Flags
are disclosures, not failures.

## The 10:62 opener repair (one seat, prescribed by the checker)

The checker graded 10:62 PARTIAL: the substance was carried, but
the opener was awkward, arguably inverted. It was repaired in the
same correction pass, the rest of the verse and its Arabic line
untouched:

- Before: **10:62** — "He ʻoiaʻiʻo ʻole, ʻaʻohe mea e makaʻu ai
  nā hoa o Allah, ʻaʻole hoʻi lākou e kaumaha."
- After: **10:62** — "He ʻoiaʻiʻo, ʻaʻohe mea e makaʻu ai nā hoa
  o Allah, ʻaʻole hoʻi lākou e kaumaha."

("He ʻoiaʻiʻo ʻole" reads literally as "it is untrue"; the repair
restores the affirmative opener, "He ʻoiaʻiʻo.")

adds or removes content, and the inconsistency is carried as
disclosed, not silently unified at the desk.

## The Purple tooling caveat

The house Purple measurer (~/workspace/.tools/purple_measure.py)
is hard-wired to 19 slice-structured lanes and cannot measure
this range-structured lane: its measure() requires inputs
(work/meta.json, frontmatter and titles files, slice files) that
this lane does not have. Purple's measures were therefore applied
directly to the manuscript in REPORT mode only, and the sweep
stands in the lane record as PURPLE-SWEEP-HAW-REPORT-2026-11-22.md.
No APPLY was run. Purple identifies candidate mechanical flaws
only; it does not judge speaker, fluency, or meaning. Its G7
English function-word measure produced 5,033 candidate hits —
"he" 2,547 and "no" 2,486, every other listed English function
word 0 — reproduced exactly by the independent checker as the
ordinary Hawaiian words he (the indefinite article) and no (the
preposition/particle): false positives, reconciled. Its G8
measures were clean: "%" 0, TODO 0, GAP markers 0, empty verses 0.
Its one real finding was the dropped 13:15 prostration note,
restored as disclosed above.
