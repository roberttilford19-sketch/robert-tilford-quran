# GLOSSARY-SCO — Scots lane locked and recurring term choices

Declared norm: **Modern literary Scots**, disciplined by Dictionaries of the
Scots Language (DSL) spellings and usage, broad Lowland literary base.
No apologetic apostrophes anywhere (write *o*, never *o'*).
Counts are whole-build occurrences in the Scots slices (verses only).

## Sustained Scots grammar (the lane's test)

- Negation: *dinna* 609, *willna* 244, *didna* 106, *doesna* 98, *isna* 71,
  *canna* 65, *wadna* 50, *wisna* 23, *haena* 18, *couldna* 15, *shouldna* 6;
  adverb *no*; determiner *nae* 834.
- Pronouns: *ye* 4,476, *thaim* 3,420, *thay* 2,839, *thair* 1,243,
  *yer* 1,145, *thairsels*; relative *wha* 1,898.
- Core prepositions/forms: *an* 7,243, *o* 3,902, *tae* 3,278, *frae* 1,132,
  *wi* 1,093, *amang* 283, *agin* 167, *atween* 141, *afore* 301, *efter* 172.
- Verbs: *hae* 1,186, *haes* 579, *dae* 497, *gie* 191, *ken* 270, *tak* 115,
  *mak* 134, *gang* 64, *gane* 20.
- Weak past in *-it* (sample pattern): *creatit* 164, *blessit* 41, etc.
- Gerunds in *-in*: *believin, forgivin, enterin* (sample pattern).
