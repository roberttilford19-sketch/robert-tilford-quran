# 章 001-004

Verses where Tilford's English was ambiguous or required a judgment call. Per the hard rule, each was translated plainly as written, without resolving ambiguity via outside knowledge.

- **3:7** — "seeking descent" (following the unspecific verses, "seeking descent, and seeking to derive an interpretation"). Unusual phrasing; translated plainly as 寻求降临. Not resolved via outside knowledge.
- **2:163** — Tilford writes "the Benevolent, the Compassionate" instead of his usual "the Beneficent, the Merciful". Rendered 至仁、至慈 to keep the divine-name pair consistent per the terminology table.
- **2:186** — "I Am near" (capitalized "Am"). Rendered plainly as 我是近的; no theological gloss added.
- **3:138** — "God's coloring." Rendered 真主的色彩 following Tilford's wording exactly (logged in glossary).
- **4:34** — "then strike them." Translated plainly as written (然后打她们); no softening, no elaboration.
- **2:54** — "kill your egos." Translated plainly as 杀死你们的自我 per the English.
- **2:104** — "Do not say ambiguous words." Translated plainly as 不要说模棱两可的话.
- **2:187** — "They are a garment for you, and you are a garment for them." Metaphor kept literal: 她们是你们的衣服，你们是她们的衣服.
- **2:256** — "the most trustworthy handle; which does not break." Rendered 最牢固的把柄，不会断裂 per the English.
- **3:154** — "Is anything up to us?" Rendered 有我们什么事吗？ following the English.
- **3:55** — "I am terminating your life." Rendered 我要结束你的生命 per the English.
- **2:210** — "Are they waiting for God Himself to come to them in the shadows of the clouds, together with the angels." Rendered plainly as written.
- **4:157** — "but it appeared to them as if they did." Rendered 但那在他们看来好像 per the English.
- **4:15** — "confine them to the homes until death claims them, or God makes a way for them." Rendered plainly as written.
- **4:24** — "except those you rightfully possess." Rendered 除非你们合法拥有; no elaboration.
- **2:222** — The English carries a stray extra closing quotation mark at the end; treated as a typo and not reproduced.
- **2:243 / 2:251 / 3:152** — Tilford's "Gracious" rendered 仁慈的, kept distinct from 至仁 (Beneficent) and 至慈 (Merciful) per his wording.
- **2:37 / 2:160 / 4:16 / 4:64** — Tilford's "Relenting", "Acceptor of Repentance", and "Redeemer" all rendered 至宥的 (one Arabic root); see glossary.
- **1:5** — "It is You we worship, and upon You we call for help." The exclusive emphasis rendered as 我们只崇拜你，我们只求你帮助.
- **4:76** — "fight in the cause of Evil." "Evil" rendered 恶魔 (taghut); see glossary.
- **4:127** — "widowed women from whom you withhold what is decreed for them" — dense English rendered plainly as 关于寡妇的——你们扣留给她们规定的.
- **2:282** — Very long legal verse; split into clause-faithful Chinese sentences in the same order, no content added or dropped.
- **4:11–12 / 4:176** — Inheritance fractions rendered exactly as the English states them (two-thirds, one-half, one-sixth, one-third, one-fourth, one-eighth).

## Surah 5
- **5:2** — "the garlanded" (collared sacrificial animals) rendered 系项圈的供品; "in pilgrim sanctity" rendered 受戒; "God's sacraments" rendered 真主的圣仪.
- **5:6** — Variant-reading note preserved from the English manuscript as an italic note ("*[有些读法是抹到脚为止；这里印刷的读法是洗。]*"), placed inside the 5:6 block exactly as in the source; it is a translator's note, not verse text.
- **5:12** — English "the alms" rendered 施舍 (not 天课) per the explicit project ruling; 5:55 "give charity" likewise 施舍 for consistency with the English word "charity".
- **5:23** — "God is the Dispenser of grace" plain rendering; no doctrinal elaboration added.
- **5:29** — "I want you to bear my sin and your sin" rendered plainly as reciprocal phrasing in the English suggests.
- **5:30** — "His soul prompted him" rendered 他的私欲怂恿他 ("his desire instigated him"), following the English as written.
- **5:57** — English "And obey God" rendered 服从真主; the earlier draft's 敬畏 was corrected.
- **5:45** — "if anyone forgoes it as charity" rendered 谁施舍放弃报复 (forgoing retaliation as a charitable act).
- **5:48** — "confirming the Scripture that preceded it, and superseding it" rendered 证实它之前的经典，并监护它 (the English "superseding" kept as监护 "guarding/overseeing").
- **5:60** — "idol worshipers" rendered 偶像崇拜者; "apes and pigs" kept literal per the English.
- **5:66** — "they would have eaten from above them and from beneath their feet" kept literal; no metaphorical gloss.
- **5:97** — English "the Kaabah, the Sacred House" rendered 天房——圣殿, keeping 圣殿 distinct from 禁寺 (the Sacred Mosque).
- **5:103** — Bahirah, Saibah, Wasilah, Hami transliterated (巴希拉、萨伊白、瓦西拉、哈米) since the English gives no definitions; readers get the transliteration only.
- **5:105–107** — Oath/will procedure rendered literally from the English; the two-witnesses mechanism follows the English wording without legal elaboration.
- **5:110** — "the Holy Spirit" rendered 圣灵.

- **6:8–9** — "If We had sent an angel… We would have made him a man" — pronouns follow the English ("him"), which is ambiguous between the angel and the messenger.
- **6:38** — English "We neglected nothing in the Scripture" rendered 我们在经典中没有遗漏任何事物 ("the Scripture" → 经典, distinct from "the Book" → 这部经典).
- **6:47** — "suddenly or gradually" rendered 突然或逐渐.
- **6:53** — "Is it these whom God has blessed among us?" rendered plainly as quoted speech of the disbelievers.
- **6:57** — "the decision is God's alone" plain rendering.
- **6:60** — "He takes your souls by night" rendered plainly.
- **6:67** — "Every tiding has its appointed time" plain rendering.
- **6:73** — "Knower of secrets and declarations" rendered 知晓秘密与公开的.
- **6:85–86** — The English distinguishes "Elias" (6:85) from "Elijah" (6:86); the Chinese preserves this as 伊利亚斯 vs 以利亚, flagged as a deliberate source distinction, not an inconsistency.
- **6:92** — "Mother of Cities" rendered 众城之母.
- **6:98** — "a place of settlement and a repository" rendered plainly.
- **6:100** — "they attribute to Him sons and daughters" kept literal per the English.
- **6:105** — "so that they may say, 'You have studied'" rendered plainly.
- **6:109** — "Signs are only with God" plain rendering.
- **6:122** — "was dead and We gave him life" rendered plainly as a parable image.
- **6:125** — The heart "narrow, constricted, as if he were climbing into the sky" kept as a physical metaphor exactly as in the English; no interpretation added.
- **6:158** — "until the angels arrive, or for your Lord to arrive" rendered plainly (你的主降临); no gloss added.
- **6:128** — The jinn's speech and God's reply rendered with pronouns following the English.
- **6:131** — "your Lord would never destroy towns for their wrongdoing while their people are unaware" plain rendering.
- **6:141–145** — The dietary-law passage follows the English wording throughout ("cultivated and wild" 耕种的和野生的; "for transportation and for clothing" 作交通的/作衣料的; "without being deliberate or malicious" 非故意、非恶意).
- **6:154** — "perfect for the righteous" rendered 完美地赐给敬畏者.

## Surah 7
- **7:73** — English "Thamood" transliterated 赛莫德 (not the more common 塞姆德), following the English spelling.
- **7:54** — "He established Himself on the Throne" rendered 升上宝座 ("ascended the throne"), following the English without commentary.
- **7:143** — God's manifestation to the mountain kept literal (当他的主向山显现，它被夷为平地); no interpretation added.
- **7:172** — "He summoned the descendants of Adam" rendered plainly (他召唤阿丹的后裔).
- **7:182** — "I will gradually lead them from where they do not know" rendered 逐渐引导他们 ("gradually guide them"); the English wording is unusual and the rendering follows it exactly. Flagged for Robert's review.
- **7:189** — "that he may find comfort with her" rendered 以便他能从她那里得安慰 (comfort found with the mate, not given by him).
- **7:189–190** — Pronoun shifts (you/they, he/you) follow the English as written; no smoothing.
- **7:202** — "their brethren lead them relentlessly into error" rendered 他们的弟兄引他们不断陷入迷误; pronouns follow the English.
- **7:206** — Prostration marker preserved from the source as *[叩头——推荐]*; it is a recitation note, not verse text.

## Surah 8
- **8:17** — "it was not you who launched when you launched, but it was God who launched" rendered literally (不是你投掷……而是真主投掷); no softening.
- **8:25** — "discord" rendered 内乱; **8:39** "persecution" rendered 迫害 — Tilford's differing English kept distinct, matching the 4:91 内乱 precedent.
- **8:41** — "the relatives" rendered 近亲 following the English (no specification of whose relatives).
- **8:60** — "all the cavalry you can mobilize" rendered 你们所能调动的骑兵 (mobilize captured; no Arabic-derived "tethered horses" gloss).

## Surah 9
- **9:3** — "the Greater Pilgrimage" rendered 大朝.
- **9:5** — The commands to kill/capture/besiege the polytheists translated directly without softening or added commentary.
- **9:12** — "they have no faith" rendered plainly (他们没有信仰), following the English as written.
- **9:29** — "the due tax, willingly or unwillingly" rendered 丁税……无论情愿与否; the full "willingly or unwillingly" distinction is kept.
- **9:30** — "Ezra" transliterated 欧宰尔 (not 以斯拉); "the Messiah" transliterated 麦西哈 (not 弥赛亚). Both are flagged: the transliterations follow the English proper nouns rather than established Chinese biblical equivalents, since the project rule is to translate Tilford's English.
- **9:61** — "He is all ears" rendered 他是耳朵, plainly as written.
- **9:111** — "God has purchased from the believers their lives and their properties" rendered literally (真主确已从信士那里购买他们的生命和财产); no theological gloss.
- **9:112** — "those who journey" rendered 旅行的人, plainly as written.
- **9:117** — "He is Kind towards them, Compassionate" rendered 他对他们是仁爱的、慈悯的.
- **9:118** — "the three who were left behind" kept plain; "He redeemed them, so that they may repent" rendered 他赦宥他们，以便他们忏悔.
- **9:129** — "the Lord of the Sublime Throne" rendered 崇高宝座的主.
- **9:53** — "it will not be accepted from you" rendered 都不会被接受 (accepted *from* them, not *by* them).
- **9:60** — "for reconciling hearts" rendered 被团结心的人 (those whose hearts are to be reconciled); "a duty from God" rendered 来自真主的义务.
- **9:78** — The verse is a rhetorical question in the English ("Do they not know…? And that God is the Knower of the unseen?"); both question marks are kept.

## Cross-cutting decisions
- **"the righteous"** — All 15 English occurrences (5:27, 5:46, 5:84, 5:85, 6:84, 6:154, 7:128, 7:161, 7:196, 9:4, 9:36, 9:44, 9:91, 9:120, 9:123) render 敬畏者, kept distinct from 行善者 ("doer of good") and 正直者 ("the upright").
- **Names** — Personal names use biblical-style forms (大卫, 所罗门, 以撒, 雅各, 约伯, 约拿, 罗得, 撒迦利亚, 以实玛利, 舒艾卜, 萨利赫), matching the brief-fixed 摩西/耶稣/亚伯拉罕/挪亚 set; 哈伦, 胡德, 麦尔彦, 优素福 unchanged. This differs from the Ma Jian-style forms in most other ranges — flagged for the manuscript-wide harmonization pass (see main GLOSSARY-ZH.md §7).
- **"charity" vs "alms"** — English "charity/charities" → 施舍; English "the alms"/"the obligatory charity" → 天课, except 5:12 "the alms" → 施舍 per the explicit project ruling.
- **Basmalah** — Translated once per surah heading for Surahs 5–8 as *奉至仁至慈的真主之名。*, matching the zh-001-004 rendering; Surah 9 has none, matching the source.
- **No outside sources** were used to resolve ambiguity; the English was translated plainly throughout.
