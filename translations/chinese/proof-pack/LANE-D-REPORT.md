# LANE D REPORT — Chinese Ayah Companion, Verse-Level Review, Surahs 58–114

**Scope:** Surahs 58–114 (57 surahs, 1,254 verses), read in full, CN against Robert's English Recital master and the lane's locked GLOSSARY-ZH.md. Report only — no manuscript file was edited.
**Sources:** CN staging anchor `robert-tilford-quran-staging/translations/chinese/ayah-companion-manuscript-ZH.md`; EN `original-recital/files/original-recital-FULL.txt`; candidate flags from `census-flags.json`.
**Standards:** The English is the sole source. Glossary-fixed terms bind: 真主 (God), 礼拜 (salat), 天课 (zakat — never 施舍）, 这部经典 ("the Book"), 火狱 (Hell), 乐园 (Paradise), 信道者 / 不信道者， 敬畏者 (the righteous), 以物配主者 (mushrikun — expressly *not* 多神教徒）, biblical-style names.

## 2. Lane D's dominant pattern: divine "We" flattened to "我" (Class 1/5)

The census rule caught only 2 of these. A paired sizing pass (EN divine We/Our/Us, CN with bare 我 and no 我们） returns **47 verses** in Surahs 58–114; I read every one in context and the pattern holds in each. Two Surah 2 instances (2:3, 2:57) are thus the visible tip of a book-wide habit: wherever Robert's English keeps God's royal plural, the Chinese narration repeatedly speaks in the singular.

## 3. Headline single find: 81:4 follows a different source reading (Class 2/5)

> **81:4** EN (Recital master): "And when **the pregnant camels are left untended**."
> **81:4** CN: "当**亲密关系被搁置**。" ("When intimate relationships are set aside.")

The Chinese does not translate the master's verse at all; it renders the alternate "relationships suspended" reading that circulates in the other lineage of Robert's text. Whatever its merits as interpretation, against the sole-source rule this is the lane's starkest single-verse divergence: a whole clause replaced by a different tradition's reading. **REAL, high confidence.**

## 4. "Signs" has no stable carrier (Class 4)

Robert's "signs" (ayat) comes out as 启示 ("revelations") in **7 verses**: 62:2 "向他们诵读他的**启示**" (EN "recite His **signs**"), 62:5 "否认真主**启示**的民众" (EN "denied God's signs"), 64:10 "否认我的**启示**" (EN "denied Our signs"), 74:16, 78:28 "他们彻底否认我们的**启示**" (EN "denied Our signs"), 83:13 "当我们的**启示**被诵读给他时" (EN "When Our signs are recited to him"), 90:19 "否认我们的**启示**的人" (EN "disbelieve in Our signs"). Elsewhere the same word becomes 奇迹 ("miracles": 61:6 "昭示**奇迹**" for EN "clear proofs"; 79:20 "最大的**奇迹**" for EN "the greatest sign") or 节文 (68:15). The lane never settled on 迹象， so "signs" drifts among revelation/miracle/verse depending on the slice. **REAL pattern, high confidence.**

## 5. The corrective "No —" keeps falling out (Class 1)

EN's emphatic corrective particle (kalla) is dropped at verse openings in **13 lane-D verses**, flattening a rebuke into a plain statement: 74:52 (EN "No — each one of them wants…" → CN "但他们每人都想…"), 74:54 (EN "No indeed — it is a reminder" → CN "**然而**，它是一种教诲"), 75:5, 75:14 (EN "No — man is a witness against himself" → CN drops it outright), 75:20 (EN "No — you love the fleeting life" → CN "**唉**，你们喜爱…"), 75:26, 78:4, 78:5, 81:15, 90:1, 102:3, 102:4, 102:5 (EN "No — you will soon know" → CN "你们一定会知道"). Same fault the Surah 2 audit verified at 2:214. It is kept elsewhere (74:16 绝不， 74:32/53, 89:17 "不。", 89:21 "不——", 96:6/15/19, 104:4 绝不） — again, inconsistency, not policy. **REAL pattern, high confidence.**

## 7. Category swaps and glossary drift (Class 4/5, individual)

- **98:1, 98:6 — 多神教徒 for the mushrikun. REAL.** EN: "the disbelievers among the People of the Scripture and **the idolaters**." CN: "有经人和**多神教徒**中的不信道者" (both verses). The glossary expressly fixes 以物配主者 and rejects 多神教徒 as the carrier. Positive control: 61:9 uses the fixed form — CN "即使**以物配主者**厌恶" / EN "though the idolaters hate it" ✓.
- **Judgment → 宗教 ("religion"). REAL-lite pattern ×3.** 82:9 EN "No — you deny **the Judgment**" → CN "你们否认**宗教**"; 95:7 EN "deny **the Judgment**" → CN "否认**宗教**"; 107:1 EN "the one who denies **the Judgment**" → CN "否认**宗教**的人". Ad-din in its judgment sense is re-categorized as "religion" (mirror image of 2:193, where "religion" became "worship").
- **72:14–15 — 妥协 for "the unjust." REAL (moderate).** EN: "among us are the unjust… But the unjust — they will be firewood for Hell." CN: "也有**妥协的**……但至于**妥协的**——他们将是火狱的柴" — the qasitun (unjust/deviating) become "compromisers."
- **94:5, 94:6 — "with" → "after." REAL (moderate).** The famous verses. EN: "So **with** hardship comes ease." CN: "艰难**之后**确有容易" (both verses) — the popular temporal misreading ("after hardship") replaces Robert's accompaniment ("with").
- **93:3 — despise → forget. REAL (moderate).** EN: "Your Lord has not forsaken you, **nor does He despise you**." CN: "你的主没有抛弃你，**也没有忘记**" — "despise" （厌弃） comes out as "forget."
- **59:7 — traveler → 旅客. REAL (moderate).** EN: "and the poor, and **the wayfarer**" → CN: "穷人和**旅客**" — the same modern-sociological wobble as 2:215 （旅客） and 2:177 （无家可归者）; three renderings, none literal.
- **64:17, 73:20 — "a good loan" → 优美的贷款. REAL-lite ×2.** EN: "If you lend God **a good loan**" / "lend God **a good loan**." CN: "借给真主一笔**优美的贷款**" (both). The glossary directs this exact phrase to 善债 ("直译'优美的借贷'为'善债'"); the text keeps the literal phrase the glossary told it to replace (cf. the 2:245 crew finding).
- **76:24 — disbeliever → 亵渎者. REAL-lite.** EN: "do not obey among them any sinner or **hardened disbeliever**" → CN: "不要服从他们中的犯罪者或**亵渎者**" — the category swap verified at 2:258 repeats.
- **98:8 — Eden → 永恒乐园. REAL-lite.** EN: "Gardens of Eden" → CN: "永恒乐园" ("eternal gardens"); inconsistent with 61:12, where CN correctly uses the glossary's 伊甸园.
- **90:1, 95:3 — city → 土地. Minor.** EN "this city" / "this safe city" → CN "这片土地" / "这座安宁的土地" (90:1 also drops the opening "No —", §5).

## 13. Pattern summary

Lane D confirms the Surah 2 diagnosis and sharpens it. The short Meccan surahs are lexically cleaner than Surah 2's legal stretches — no 禁寺/天房/清真寺 loans appear anywhere in 58–114 (census counts for those terms all fall in earlier lanes), no zakat breaches beyond 98:5, and the set-piece verses Robert's readers will quote most (97, 99, 103, 106, 108, 112) are faithful, 112 flawlessly so. But three habits run the whole length of the lane: (1) the divine **We flattens to 我 in 47 verses**, clustered in the Medinan and narrative surahs (73–77 are the densest) while the short oath surahs often keep 我们 — the inconsistency signature again; (2) **"signs" never found its carrier**, scattering among 启示/奇迹/节文； (3) the **corrective "No —" drops out in 13 openings**, the 2:214 fault in miniature. The single gravest verse is **81:4**, where the Chinese renders a different source reading than the master it claims to translate. Everything else is the now-familiar war: Robert's plain, literal English on one side, and the tradition's smoother, more familiar Chinese on the other — in the short surahs the English wins most verses, but it does not win them all.
