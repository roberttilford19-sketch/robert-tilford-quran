## P-03 — Divine We restoration

**Rule/warrant:** Robert's EN preserves the Arabic's divine plural ("We/Our/Us") verse by verse; where the EN speaks as "We," a CN singular 我 flattens the divine voice. **Status: READY. Patch type: VERSE-REWRITE** (each verse's clause must be re-pointed to 我们/我们的； possessives and verb frames differ per verse).

**Count conflict, on record:** the census's verb-specific patterns caught only **25** verses. The lanes' paired tests (EN divine We/Our/Us present + CN 我 with no 我们 anywhere in the verse) size the true pattern at **≈177 verses**.
