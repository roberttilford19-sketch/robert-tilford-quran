**Scope:** Surahs 29–57 (1,764 verses), staging anchor `robert-tilford-quran-staging/translations/chinese/ayah-companion-manuscript-ZH.md` against Robert's English Recital master (sole source) and the locked GLOSSARY-ZH. Report only — no manuscript file was edited.
**Method:** (1) Every census flag for surahs 29–57 (33 flags) adjudicated in context with verbatim CN + EN. (2) Beyond the census: the famous verses named in the brief (29:46, 31:12–19, 33:40, 33:72, 36:58, 39:53, 40:60, 41:34, 42:38, 49:13, 55:13) plus 30:21, 33:56, 48:29-area, 50:16, 51:56, 54:17, 56:79, 57:20, and the first 10 verses of every surah 29–57 read as drift probes (290 verse pairs). (3) Programmatic pattern scans across the lane （拜功， 启示-for-signs, 幽玄， 归顺， quote-balance state machine, name-form census).
**Alignment:** CN and EN parse 1:1 — 1,764 / 1,764 verses, zero missing. Parity is not an issue in this lane.

## §2 — Beyond-census findings, by surah

### Surah 29

## §3 — Lane-wide patterns (the sweep's real quarry)

1. **"The Book" without 这部 is the lane's dominant defect** — 22 census verses + 2 oath verses (43:2, 44:2), against only 8 correct uses of 这部经典 in 1,764 verses. Systematic, book-shaped, exactly as in surah 2.

**Pattern summary.** Lane C confirms the surah-2 diagnosis with a different center of gravity. The scaffolding is perfect (1,764/1,764, no drops, no duplications, quote faults limited to one true break at 37:149/150). The breaches concentrate in three places: (i) the two locked formulas — 这部经典 (near-absent) and the divine We (flattened in ~20 verses); (ii) one wrong worship-word, 拜功， in the set prayer formula; and (iii) ayat rendered by four different Chinese words, with 启示 ("revelations") the most frequent offender against Robert's "signs."
