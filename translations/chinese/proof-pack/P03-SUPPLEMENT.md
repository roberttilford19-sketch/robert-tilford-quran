- **Manifest P-03 rows: 248 — all applied** (247 in the first application + 38:24 in the quarantine-clearance pass; state of record = patched master v2).
- **Overlap (census-repair ∩ manifest P-03): 247.** Of the 4 MIXED verses, 2:57, 27:82 and 28:58 were manifest rows; 30:28 was not and appears below.
- **DELTA (a): 48 verses** — census-repair verses with NO manifest P-03 row, tabled below. **DELTA COUNT = 48.**
- **Reverse delta (b): 1 verse** — 28:57, a manifest P-03 row the census judges PRESERVED. Reconciliation note only (see below); **no reversal is proposed** — Robert approved that row and it is applied.
- **Base texts:** each row's Current CN is quoted verbatim from the live patched master (v3). At 44 of the 48 verses the text is byte-identical to the staging anchor (no applied row ever touched them). At 4 verses (30:28, 46:26, 64:10, 74:16) an applied P-04 row already repaired 启示→迹象； those rows are **built on the patched text** and say so, so the two repairs compose. At the 2 particle-overlap verses (70:40, 75:4) the base text is the v3 state, which already carries the applied particle repairs — the pronoun repair proposed here is the only remaining change at those verses.
- **Where the delta lives:** 7 verses in surahs 8–46 (8:59, 18:103, 29:39, 30:28, 44:30, 44:32, 46:26) and **41 in surahs 59–95** — the census's band picture (surahs 58–114 majority-flattened) and the lane reports' weakest coverage coincide here; Lane C's ≈20 for surahs 29–57 and the manifest's pairing pool both under-enumerated the late short surahs.
- **Borderline calls honored:** 51:36 and 56:61 are NOT in this supplement — the census judged them PRESERVED by cross-verse 我们 carry-over, so they never entered the 295. The census's HUMAN exclusions are likewise untouched: no creature speech, no Say:-dictated confession, no prayer addressed to God, and no royal human We is pluralized anywhere below. Two EN singulars standing inside repaired verses are deliberately preserved (68:44's "leave Me", 70:40's "I swear") and flagged in their rows.
- **Repair rule (all rows):** clause-level minimal repair of the flattened divine clauses only — 我→我们 where the EN clause carries We/Our/Us; restoration of a dropped divine object or subject where the CN dropped it; nothing else in the verse changes. Every proposed text was machine-diffed against its current text: outside the pronoun loci the two are character-identical.
