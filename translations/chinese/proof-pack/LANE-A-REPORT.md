## Part 2 — Beyond-census findings, by surah

### Surah 3 — the proper-noun surah
- **3:19 — REAL (high).** EN: "The religion with God is **surrender to Him**" → CN: 在真主那里的宗教是**伊斯兰**. Robert de-institutionalized the verse; CN re-inserts the proper noun Islam.
- **3:85 — REAL (high).** EN: "Whoever seeks a way of life other than **surrender**" → CN: 谁舍**伊斯兰**而求别的宗教. Same overwrite.
- **3:67 — REAL (high).** EN: "he was an upright monotheist, **a submitter**" → CN: 他是认一者、**穆斯林**. "Submitter" becomes the proper noun "Muslim"; "upright" also drops (cf. 2:135's 认一者 for "upright").
- **3:102 — REAL (high).** EN: "do not die except as **submitters**" → CN: 不要死，除非你们是**穆斯林**.
- **3:48, 3:65 — REAL (high).** EN "the **Gospel**" → CN **福音书** both times. The glossary fixes 引支勒； surahs 5, 7, 9 in this lane use 引支勒 (7 occurrences) — surah 3's slice alone uses 福音书 (3 occurrences incl. 3:3). Slice-level inconsistency against the locked term.
- **3:7 — REAL (high).** EN: "they chase the ambiguous part, **seeking discord** and seeking its interpretation" → CN: 他们追随不明确的部分，**寻求降临**，并寻求得出解释. 降临 ("advent/descent") is not a rendering of "discord" （纷争/挑拨） — a nonsense substitution in the famous muhkam/mutashabih verse.
- Ruling-queue instances (not new defects): 归顺 at 3:20, 3:52, 3:64, 3:80, 3:83, 3:84 (part of ×18 lane-wide, see Part 3).
- Positive: 3:59 (Jesus/Adam likeness) exact; 3:144 exact; 3:159 gentle/consult tracked; 3:190 exact.

### Surah 4
- **4:34 — REAL (moderate-high), one dropped element in an otherwise faithful verse.** EN: "Good women are devout, **guarding the unseen as God guarded**" → CN: 善良的妇女是顺从的，**守护真主让她们守护的**. The object "the unseen" disappears into a circular paraphrase. The rest of the verse tracks Robert's EN clause for clause — 劝告她们 (advise), 离弃她们于床上 (leave them alone in bed), 然后打她们 (strike them) — CN neither softens nor hardens the discipline sequence and adds no commentary.
- **4:1 — REAL (moderate).** EN: "fear God, by whom you ask one another, **and the bonds of kinship**" → CN: 敬畏真主，你们凭他相互询问，**并敬畏父母**. Al-arham (kinship bonds/wombs) becomes 父母 ("parents") — a meaning substitution in the surah's opening verse.
- **4:43 — REAL (minor).** EN "clean earth" → CN **干净的土**. The glossary (§四） fixes **洁净的土** for 4:43 and 5:6. Surah 5's slice uses the fixed form (5:6 ✓); surah 4's does not.
- Positive: 4:92 (blood-money/charity distinctions exact, 施舍 used correctly for sadaqah), 4:157 (guesswork NOT upgraded — 只追随猜测 for "only following guesswork"; negation exact), 4:171 (不要说三位； spirit-from-Him tracked), 4:3, 4:176 tracked.

## Pattern summary — Lane A in one paragraph

Surahs 3–11 repeat Surah 2's disease in a different distribution. The scaffolding is again perfect (census parity: zero failures book-wide; no dropped verses found in any read). The locked glossary's two load-bearing terms keep failing — 这部经典 is absent at every one of the lane's 14 census-flagged "the Book" verses, and zakat collapses to 施舍 at 4:77 — while two *new* mass variants appear that Surah 2 did not show: 信士 displacing 信道者 across 37 verses of the later slices, and 启示 displacing 迹象 for "signs" across 41. The theological overwriting concentrates where Robert's English is most deliberately un-institutional: six times the CN restores the proper nouns 伊斯兰/穆斯林 over his "surrender/submitters" (3:19, 3:67, 3:85, 3:102, 5:3, 6:125), and the cognition override migrates from "think" to "feel safe" and "believe" (7:97–99, 10:90). The lane's single worst artifact is mechanical, not theological: a translator's qira'at note typeset inside the text of 5:6. Against that, the lane also shows real discipline — the war and law verses sampled (4:34, 4:89, 4:92, 5:33, 5:38, 9:5, 9:29's 丁税， 9:71) track Robert's negations, agents, and categories with care, several Surah 2 pathologies （回忆当时， 同胞， 副朝） vanish entirely, and two of Surah 2's headline charges （禁寺， 多神教徒） turn out, on this lane's evidence, to be Robert's own English wording faithfully followed. The overwrite pattern is real but it is a *translator-slice* phenomenon, not a uniform one: surah 3's slice and the 8–9 slices drift hardest; surahs 10–11 are comparatively clean.
