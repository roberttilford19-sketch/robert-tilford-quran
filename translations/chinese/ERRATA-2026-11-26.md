## The correction

### 66:10

- **Before:** 她们曾在我的两位正直仆人之下 — "they were under **my** two righteous servants"
- **After:** 她们曾在我们的两位正直仆人之下 — "they were under **Our** two righteous servants"
- **What changed:** the possessive only, 我的 → 我们的. Nothing else in the verse moved.
- **Receipts:** the Arabic at the seat is *ʿibādinā*, "Our servants," plural possessive; the English master renders "two of Our righteous servants"; the lane itself renders the divine plural with 我们 at 1,031 of the 1,045 census seats. This seat was the lane's only singular.
