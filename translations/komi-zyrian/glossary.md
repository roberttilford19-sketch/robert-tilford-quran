## §B Standing GAP policy
A Master content word with no verified Komi-Zyrian seat in the
inventories takes the visible marker ⟦GAP: <english term>⟧ inline;
the rest of the verse is rendered as far as verified seats allow.
Standing unseated term families (no attested form found, or the
attestation too thin to seat): pilgrimage (technical term),
Sabbath, usury (technical term), and the long tail of low-frequency
Master vocabulary listed batch by batch in work/notes-*.md.
NEVER invent a form; NEVER borrow silently from Komi-Permyak
(koi), Komi-Yazva, Udmurt, Erzya, Moksha, Mari, Tatar, or Russian.
