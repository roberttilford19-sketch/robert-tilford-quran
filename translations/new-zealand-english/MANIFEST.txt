Status: a DIALECT EDITION of the Locked English Master — the same
translation in New Zealand English spelling and usage. Not a new
translation and not a re-translation: every word is the master's
own, changed only where the recorded conversion list
(DIALECT-CONVERSION-LIST.tsv, in this folder) changes its
spelling: 98 pairs, 388 word-forms across 6,236 verses. New Zealand
usage equals the Australian and South African conversions on every
attested pair, and the list is the South African list whole,
disclosed as such; it goes beyond the Australian list at exactly
two attested seats — meager->meagre at 34:16 and
maneuvering->manoeuvring at 8:16 — which New Zealand usage also
writes otherwise. A vocabulary scan of the master found no further
US form that New Zealand usage writes differently. Rule A and every
settled ruling of the master stand untouched in substance; the
6:86 correction (Elisha) rides as the master carries it. Sajda
notes stand at their seats, as in the master. One conversion is
seat-scoped and disclosed on the list: practice->practise at the
12 verbal seats only; the 3 nominal seats keep the noun form, as
New Zealand usage requires.
