# Udmurt Quran draft — independent checker report (2026-11-27)

Checker stance: this checker built none of the text under review. Every figure below was recomputed at the desk from the files of record: the built book `quran-udm.txt` and `quran-udm.json`, the verse store `verses-store.json`, the English master `master-verses.json`, the build spec `BUILD-SPEC.md`, the glossary, the attestation audit of 2026-11-27, and the Purple candidate list `work/purple-untraced.json`. Where a claim rests on a count, the count is stated in the same breath.

## Check 1 — Recount, seat-set, empty seats, redactions: PASS

The book is structurally complete. The built book and the verse store each hold 6,236 verses across 114 surahs, and the English master holds 6,236 across 114. Every per-surah count in both the master and the built book matches the canonical grid exactly (7, 286, 200, 176, 120, 165, 206, 75, 129, and so on to 6 at surah 114); there is not one surah whose count differs. The seat sets are identical: master seats, built seats, and store seats are the same 6,236 seats with zero symmetric difference, and the store's text equals the built JSON's text at every seat, with zero value mismatches. There are no empty seats in either file, and no seat in the Udmurt text contains the word "redacted" (zero seats). In the `.txt` rendering, the numbered verse sequence inside every surah runs unbroken from 1 to that surah's canonical count, with no gaps, duplicates, or misnumbering in any of the 114 surahs. For scale: 1,759 of the 6,236 verses carry at least one GAP marker, and there are 2,534 GAP markers in total.

One labelling failure belongs in this section because it concerns the built book as delivered. The spec's Honesty clause requires "DRAFT — NOT PUBLISHED" on the book's face. The delivered `quran-udm.txt` has no front matter at all: it opens directly with the heading of surah 1, and the strings "DRAFT" and "NOT PUBLISHED" occur nowhere in the file (zero occurrences). The book as built therefore does not carry the honest status its own spec requires on its face. This is a small fix, but it is a spec breach in the delivered artefact.

Verse 1:1 in the verse store is exactly «Инмарлэн нимыныз, Гажась, Гажась.», character for character, and verse 1:3 is «Гажась, Гажась.», as the spec prescribes. ## Check 3 — Orthography contamination: FAIL

Three distinct findings, of increasing weight.

First, true inside-word script contamination — Latin letters embedded inside Cyrillic words, with no hyphen to explain them — occurs at exactly four seats: 2:109 («оskemды», for оскемды), 2:272 («уjed»), 17:72 («кошkoz»), and 20:123 («кошke»). These are keyboard-layout corruptions of ordinary Udmurt words and must be repaired seat by seat.

Second, apart from those four, every Latin-letter token in the book is explained. There are 1,151 Latin-token occurrences of 142 distinct forms, and all of them are the Master's English proper names and titles carried unaltered under the names policy (Moses 136, Pharaoh 74, Satan 70, Abraham 69, Quran 68, and so on), very often with a hyphenated Udmurt case suffix, which is the policy working as designed. Only twelve pure-Latin occurrences depart from the Master's wording at their own seat: at 4:117, 12:100, 18:63, 19:44 (twice), 19:45, 19:68, 19:83, 21:82, 22:3, and 41:36 the Udmurt seats "Satan" or "Devil" where the Master at that seat says "devil" or "the Devil", and at 38:37 it seats lowercase "devil" as a common noun in Latin letters. The Master's own Satan/devil distinction is therefore not carried unaltered at those twelve seats, contrary to the names policy.

Third, and materially, the Russian-only letters ф, х, ц, щ — none of which belongs to the Udmurt alphabet — occur 88 times across 69 verses in 34 distinct word forms. Six occurrences are ф (финик "dates" at 13:4, 16:11, 16:67, 55:68, 80:29, and Катастрофа at 79:34), 34 are х, 5 are ц, and 43 are щ. The bulk is not stray colour vocabulary: it is two Russian religious stems seated wholesale in one stretch of the book. The stem прощ- (Russian "forgive") occurs 41 times in 31 verses, and грех (Russian "sin") 28 times in 24 verses, every one of those verses in surahs 4–6 (seats 4:31–7:33), including core seats such as 4:48, 4:116, and 5:3. The remaining forms are Russian common nouns seated without comment: падчерица (4:23), Храм (17:7), христианъёс (22:17), Дворец/дворец (27:44), пастухъёс (28:23), сахар (16:67), горчица (31:16), матрацъёсын (56:34), пастбище (79:31, 87:4), and Эх (75:20, 78:40). None of these forms appears in the glossary's attested seats or in the attestation audit's recorded loan layers, so against the lane's own record they stand as silently seated Russian forms, which the spec forbids in terms ("Russian forms are NEVER silently seated").

On normalised look-alikes, the verdict is reassuring in one direction and must be stated precisely. The five special Udmurt letters are genuinely and heavily used, not globally normalised away: ӝ 314 occurrences, ӟ 973, ӥ 8,583, ӧ 4,094, ӵ 542, with capitals Ӝ 20, Ӟ 10, Ӧ 27, Ӵ 15. The capital Ӥ occurs zero times, but no sentence in the book needs to open with an Ӥ-initial word, so that zero proves nothing by itself. The digraphs дж and дз, which would be the fingerprint of normalising Ӝ and Ӟ, do not appear as Udmurt digraphs; the one дж-word found is the whole Russian infinitive «блуждать» seated at 2:15 ("блуждать карыса"), which is counted under the silent-loan findings in Check 5 rather than here. Base letters ж (2,165), з (18,355), and ч (1,224) are themselves legitimate Udmurt letters, so their presence is not evidence of normalisation.

## Check 4 — Clause fidelity sample (36 verses, three per batch): FAIL on one seat, with lesser deviations

The sample was 36 verses, three from each of the twelve batch ranges, chosen to include long legal and narrative verses: A — 2:255, 2:282, 3:7; B — 4:11, 5:6, 6:151; C — 9:111, 10:35, 11:41; D — 12:100, 14:4, 16:90; E — 18:25, 20:130, 21:107; F — 24:2, 24:31, 25:63; G — 28:88, 30:21, 33:50; H — 36:58, 38:71, 39:53; I — 42:23, 45:21, 49:13; J — 55:26, 57:20, 59:7; K — 65:1, 71:28, 76:8; L — 89:17, 99:1, 104:1. Each Udmurt rendering was compared clause by clause against the English master at the same seat. A further spot battery of number verses (2:261, 7:142, 8:65, 12:4, 37:147) was checked outside the 36.

The general picture is strong. Thirty of the thirty-six are clause-complete: nothing dropped, nothing added, no reversal. The long legal verses are the best evidence — 2:282 (the debt verse, the longest in the book) carries every clause including the spot-transaction exception and the harm-to-scribe-or-witness clause; 24:31 carries the full kin list without dropping a category; 33:50 and 65:1 are likewise complete; and the inheritance fractions at 4:11 are all numerically right (two-thirds, one-half, one-sixth, one-third each rendered correctly).

One material error fails this check. At 18:25 the Master reads "three hundred years, adding nine"; the Udmurt reads «куинь сю ар улӥзы, куать но ватсаса» — three hundred years, adding **six** (куать), not nine (укмыс). A number in a famous narrative verse is wrong, and the seat must be corrected. The spot battery found the other tested numbers right (thirty, ten, and forty nights at 7:142; seven ears and a hundred grains at 2:261; a hundred thousand at 37:147; twenty, two hundred, a hundred, and a thousand at 8:65), which makes 18:25 an isolated error rather than a pattern, but it is a material one.

Lesser deviations, seat by seat, none of them a dropped clause or a reversal: at 59:7 "the relatives" is rendered эшъёс, "friends/companions", which changes who receives; at 12:4 (spot battery) the Master's "eleven planets" becomes "eleven stars" (кизили); at 11:41 the epithet "Forgiving" is rendered Куштӥсь, from the stem кушт- that the same book uses at 3:21 for denying the revelations, which reads as a wrong epithet at that seat; at 24:2 the Last Day is spelled «Бӧрысь Нунал», a garbled form of the Берпул/Берло Нунал seat; at 4:11 "siblings" is narrowed to братъёсыз, "brothers"; at 49:13 "tribes" becomes семьяос, "families"; at 39:53 "all sins" becomes "all faults" (янгышъёс) and "the Clement" becomes «Каллен улӥсь», both suspect; at 14:4 "the Wise" is rendered Тодос, a form used nowhere else for that epithet; at 12:100 "the wilderness" is rendered with the Russian noun степь; at 3:7 "foundation of the Book" is weakened to "place of the Book"; at 20:130 "that you may be satisfied" is weakened to "that you may be well". At 5:6 the Udmurt matches the master of record exactly; the master verse at that seat is itself short, and the checker judges against the master as given, so no fault is recorded against the translation there.

## Check 5 — GAP practice: FAIL

The standing GAP terms pass completely. Jinn is GAP-marked at every one of its 26 master verses (32 markers including repeats); pilgrimage is GAP-marked at all four term seats (2:158, 3:97, 9:3, 22:27) and also where the Master says "Hajj" at 2:196; Sabbath is GAP-marked at all five seats (2:65, 4:47, 4:154, 7:163, 16:124); usury is GAP-marked at all six seats (2:275, 2:276, 2:278, 3:130, 4:161, 30:39); and fasting, also held as GAP by the audit, is GAP-marked wherever it occurs as a term. No standing GAP term was found silently seated anywhere.

The prophet seat also passes in its narrow terms. Пайгамбар (92 occurrences, 86 verses, counting capitalised forms) is seated at every one of the 75 master verses that name a prophet, with no exceptions; the abstract noun "prophethood" is GAP-marked at 6:89 and 29:27, which is consistent GAP practice rather than a deviation. The exact forbidden periphrasis string «Иньмарлэн ыстэм муртэз» occurs zero times, and пророк occurs zero times. Two qualifications must ride with that pass. The same periphrasis in standard spelling, «Инмарлэн ыстэм муртэз» — "God's sent man" — is the book's standing rendering of "Messenger of God" at thirteen seats (9:81, 9:120, 33:21, 33:40, 48:29, 49:3, 49:7, 61:5, 61:6, 63:1, 63:5, 63:7, 91:13), and the shorter periphrasis ыстэм мурт, "sent man", renders "messenger" at 259 of the 318 master verses that contain the word. The glossary calls that phrase an invention and forbids it as a coined seat; it is not used for "prophet", but it is seated, undisclosed in the glossary and the audit, as the book's messenger term. A remediation ruling is needed on whether ыстэм мурт stands as a disclosed compositional rendering or falls under the invention ban; the checker records the fact and does not rule.

Batch-level GAP practice fails in both directions the task named.

In the low-GAP batches, core religious terms are silently seated in Russian forms instead of being GAP-marked or rendered from the glossary. Batch J carries only 50 GAP markers in 520 verses (7.7 percent of verses) and batch L only 2 markers in 516 verses (0.4 percent), yet sampling those batches finds: Святой, the Russian adjective "holy", seated as the divine epithet at 59:23 and 62:1 in batch J and in lowercase at 79:16 in batch L, while batch E seats the same concept at 16:102 as Сӥлы Лул and batch B GAP-marks it at 5:110; проклят-, the Russian curse stem, seated at 111:1 (twice) and 81:25 in batch L, while "curse/cursed" is GAP-marked 31 times elsewhere in the book; колдунство, Russian "sorcery", seated at 113:4 in batch L, while magic and sorcery are GAP-marked elsewhere; верблюд, Russian "camel", seated at 54:27, 56:55, and 59:6 in batch J and at 88:17 and 91:13 in batch L; весы, Russian "scales", seated at 101:6 and 101:8 in batch L (and at 17:35 in batch E), while "measure" is GAP-marked twelve times elsewhere; and столб, Russian "pillar", seated at 89:7 and 104:9 in batch L. Batch H (75 markers, 13.5 percent of verses) is cleaner in sampling — its random sample showed native renderings and honest GAPs — but it shares the book-wide inconsistencies recorded in Check 6. The near-zero GAP rates of batches J and L are therefore not evidence of fuller attestation; they are, in part, unmarked borrowing.

In high-GAP batch B, the opposite fault is proven against the book's own practice. Batch B carries 647 GAP markers in 520 verses (61.3 percent of verses GAP-bearing, against a book average of 28.2 percent), across 322 distinct GAP terms. It GAP-marks words that the book itself seats elsewhere: "messenger/messengers" 62 times (34 singular, 28 plural, seats 4:42–7:77) while 259 verses elsewhere seat ыстэм мурт and twelve verses in surahs 24–25 seat пайгамбар for the same English word; "garden/gardens" 15 times (including the Garden verses 7:19, 7:22, 7:27) while сад is seated from 2:35 onward, including at 54:54 in batch J; "witness/witnesses/testimony" 31 times while batch A seats свидетель 27 times in 19 verses (2:23–4:6) — itself a Russian loan, so the concept is rendered in A, GAP-marked in B, and rendered again by other means later; "wise" 15 times and "wisdom" 4 times while batch I seats визь for wisdom at 45:16 and batch A seats a native epithet at 2:255's close in paraphrase. Whatever the correct attestation ruling on each word, a book cannot both seat a rendering and GAP-mark the same concept without a recorded reason, and no reason is recorded. Batch B is over-marked relative to the book's own settled practice.

A further inconsistency belongs here because it is a term-seat failure: "messenger" is rendered three different ways across the book — GAP at 48 verses (all in surahs 4–7), ыстэм мурт at 259 verses, and пайгамбар, the prophet seat, at twelve verses in surahs 24–25 (24:47, 24:48, 24:50, 24:51, 24:52, 24:54, 24:56, 24:62, 24:63, 25:27, 25:30, and 22:78's closing clause), where "God and His Messenger" becomes "God and His Prophet". That conflates two distinct core terms at those seats. Conversely, batch J GAP-marks the proper name itself at 58:10 and 58:19 (⟦GAP: Satan⟧), while Satan is seated in Latin at seventy other verses — over-marking of a name the names policy says to carry unaltered.

Finally, the scale of untraced seating is conceded by the build's own Purple list: `work/purple-untraced.json` holds 5,396 untraced word types totalling 29,344 occurrences, and it contains the very forms this report flags (грех, прощась, свидетель-type forms, the four script-contaminated words). The attestation audit's statement that no Russian form was silently seated does not survive contact with that list or with Checks 3 and 5 of this report.

## Check 6 — Core-term consistency: PASS on the named seats, with three recorded splits

The eight named seats were tested against every master verse containing the corresponding English term. God = Инмар is exceptionless: all 1,836 master verses containing "God" carry Инмар (2,886 occurrences in 1,891 verses). Lord is exceptionless in its glossary form "Кузё / Инмар in context": all 863 master verses containing "Lord" carry Кузё or Инмар (Кузё alone: 1,017 occurrences). Angel = кылчин is exceptionless: all 86 master angel verses carry кылчин. Spirit = лул appears at all 19 master verses containing "spirit" (лул: 147 occurrences). Prayer = вӧсь and its вӧсяськ- stem appear at all 133 master pray- verses except 12:97, where "pray for the forgiveness of our sins" is rendered as a direct request for forgiveness (гажан куры) with no prayer word — a paraphrase, not a wrong term. Paradise = рай appears at all 33 master Paradise verses (89:30's «Раям» is рай in the dative, capitalised). Hell = ад appears at all 91 master Hell verses, with no exceptions. Judgement Day in the strict sense is exact: all seven master "Day of Judgment" verses (1:4, 15:35, 37:20, 38:78, 51:12, 70:26, 74:46) carry Суд нунал, and Суд нунал occurs 62 times in 61 verses overall.

Three splits must be recorded against the consistency standard, because a reader meets one concept under several seats. First, the Master's "Day of Resurrection" (68 verses) is rendered four ways: Суд нунал at 42 verses, Улӟон Нунал at ten verses (all in batch A: 2:85, 2:113, 2:174, 2:212, 3:55, 3:77, 3:161, 3:180, 3:185, 3:194), ⟦GAP: resurrection⟧ plus Нунал at nine verses (all in batch B: 4:87, 4:109, 4:141, 4:159, 5:14, 5:36, 5:64, 6:12, 7:32), and ⟦GAP: Resurrection⟧ plus нунал at seven verses (all in batch H: 35:14, 39:15, 39:24, 39:31, 39:47, 39:60, 39:67). The Judgment seat itself is clean, but the eschatological day as a whole is not seated consistently. Second, the Holy Spirit is rendered three ways: Святой Лул at 2:87 and 2:253 (Russian adjective), ⟦GAP: holy spirit⟧ at 5:110, and Сӥлы Лул at 16:102, with plain Лул at 26:193, 70:4, 78:38, and 97:4. Third, "sin" is rendered three ways: the native сьӧлык (for example at 12:97), the Russian грех at 28 occurrences in batch B, and янгыш, "error/fault", at 39:53. The messenger/prophet conflation at the twelve surah 24–25 seats, recorded in Check 5, is also a core-term deviation and is counted there.

1. Script contamination at four seats: 2:109, 2:272, 17:72, 20:123 (Check 3).
2. Russian-only letters and silently seated Russian stems: прощ- at 31 verses and грех at 24 verses in surahs 4–6, plus the scattered forms listed in Check 3 at 4:23, 13:4, 16:11, 16:67, 17:7, 22:17, 27:44, 28:23, 31:16, 55:68, 56:34, 75:20, 78:40, 79:31, 79:34, 80:29, 87:4 (Check 3); свидетель at 19 verses in batch A and наказани- at 33 verses in batch B (Check 5).

4. Silent Russian seats in low-GAP batches J and L: Святой at 59:23, 62:1, 79:16; проклят- at 81:25, 111:1; колдунство at 113:4; верблюд at 54:27, 56:55, 59:6, 88:17, 91:13; весы at 101:6, 101:8 (and 17:35); столб at 89:7, 104:9 (Check 5).
5. Batch B over-marking against the book's own seated renderings: messenger/messengers 62 markers, garden/gardens 15, witness group 31, wise/wisdom 19 (Check 5).
6. Messenger seated three ways (GAP 48 verses; ыстэм мурт 259; пайгамбар at twelve surah 24–25 seats), and the undisclosed ыстэм мурт periphrasis standing as the messenger seat, including «Инмарлэн ыстэм муртэз» at thirteen "Messenger of God" seats (Check 5).
7. Resurrection Day seated four ways across batches A, B, and H, and Holy Spirit seated three ways (Check 6).
8. The delivered `quran-udm.txt` carries no "DRAFT — NOT PUBLISHED" label on its face, against the spec's Honesty clause (Check 1).
9. Lesser fidelity deviations at 59:7, 12:4, 11:41, 24:2, 39:53, and 14:4 (Check 4), and the Satan/devil name-form departures at twelve seats (Check 3).

Checks 1 and 2 pass outright, the standing GAP terms and the prophet seat pass, and the eight named core seats pass. Checks 3, 4, and 5 fail materially, and Check 6 carries recorded splits that a remediation crew must unify. The draft is structurally complete and, in the sampled verses, usually clause-faithful; it is not yet clean on its own spec's central promises — no silently seated Russian forms, no invented seats, one consistent seat per core term.
