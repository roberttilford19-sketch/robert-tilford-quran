## Coverage note (brief §9.5)
The harvested open lexica are thin against Qur'anic vocabulary
(Wiktionary: 865 lemmas; GiellaLT stems are the deep inventory at
41,805 entries including compounds and proper nouns). The build
therefore leans on the GiellaLT inventory for attestation checking in
verify.py, seats the core terms above, and GAP-marks or descriptively
renders the rest — the gap census is reported with the build, in the
same breath as the verse count.
