## 2. Terminology decisions for the native reviewer

- **God = Gott throughout; body "Allah" count = 0** (verified in the assembled file and in every slice text). Per the brief, this is flagged for the reviewer: many German readers may expect "Allah". "Muhammad" is kept as in the English (4 verse-body occurrences; surah 47 title also Muhammad); "Imran" kept.
- **"The Reminder" = "die Ermahnung"** fixed by the brief and held across slices (Ermahnung appears 47× in verse text; the exact phrase "die Ermahnung" 5×) — the Urdu two-renderings weak spot did not recur.
- **Divine epithets vary where the slices met "Compassionate" outside the fixed pair:** the brief fixed the Beneficent = "der Wohltätige" and the Merciful = "der Barmherzige" (basmalah: "Im Namen Gottes, des Wohltätigen, des Barmherzigen."), but "the Compassionate" was rendered "der Barmherzige" at 55:1 and 59:22, "der Wohltätige" at 67:3, and the pair at 59:22 came out "der Barmherzige, der Erbarmer" (the single "Erbarmer" in the draft). A German reviewer should decide one Rahman/Rahim convention and normalize. Term counts across verse text: Wohltätig* 62, Barmherzig* 232, Erbarm* 1.
- **jinn = Dschinn is normed** (32× in verse text, no stray variants) — one of the cleaner terminology runs in the project.
- **People-name forms are mostly established German forms** (Abraham, Mose, Jesus, Maria; Ismael/Elischa/Ezechiel where flagged), with one live spelling split: **Schuaib 9× vs Schuʿaib 2×** (26:177, 29:36). Needs one project-wide ruling. Thamud (26×), Salih (9×), Hud (7×) and Midian (10×) are consistent; Quaroon was kept in the English spelling at 40:24 (flagged) while Hamaan was normalized to Haman (6×).
- **zakat/almsgiving:** the obligatory charity appears as "das verpflichtende Almosen" in some slices and "das regelmäßige Almosen" in others, with plain "Almosen" (15×) and "Wohltätigkeit" (32×) for charity generally — **no percentage anywhere** (verified 0 "%" signs in body and file), per the Quran-alone rule. A reviewer should fix one zakat term against the English "obligatory charity" occurrences.
- Fixed glossary otherwise held: Herr, Gesandter/Prophet, das Buch, der Koran, die Schrift, das Jenseits, der Garten, das Paradies, das Feuer/die Hölle, das Gebet verrichten, die Gläubigen / die Ungläubigen, die Unrechttuenden, die Heuchler, Zeichen, Tag des Gerichts / Tag der Auferstehung. Address to God is capitalized Du; plural audience is ihr; quotations use „ … “.

## 3. Literal renderings — known weak spots for a native reader

- **81:4** — "When the relationships are suspended" rendered literally: "Wenn die Beziehungen ausgesetzt werden" (flagged; reviewer may want an idiomatic alternative).
- **83:9, 83:20** — "A numerical book" rendered literally "Ein Buch mit Zahlen" (likely "ein geschriebenes Register").
- **Kept-literal items for review:** 41:10 "Stabilisatoren" über der Erde (likely mountains), 68:42 "der Unterschenkel" as a body term, 111:5 "ein Seil aus Dornen", 98:1 "waren nicht getrennt" (likely "would not cease"), 93:10 "den Suchenden" (seeker vs beggar/asker), 79:25 "in der letzten und in der ersten" (sense uncertain, flagged), and dense legal/household verses that mirror the English's heavy single sentences (e.g. 4:11–12, 33:50–53).
- **Transliterations kept as in the English** pending a norming pass: Elliyyeen (83:18) vs Elliyyoon (83:19) — the source spelling itself varies and both were kept as given (flagged); at-Tariq (86:1–2); Salsabil (76:18) beside Ingwer for Zanjabeel (76:17); idol names at 71:23 normalized by the translator to Suwa, Jaghus, Jauq, Nasr (flagged); 100:1 the English "chargers/racers" rendered with Pferden as the intended animals (flagged).
- Register variance across the 12 translators is expected. Sensitive passages (4:34, 24:2–9, 24:31–33) were translated plainly and literally — a German reviewer should check tone and legal naturalness.

## 4. Structure and discipline

- Format identical to the French/Portuguese/Italian builds: German title block (DEUTSCHES MANUSKRIPT — ÜBERARBEITUNGS-ENTWURF, keine endgültige Ausgabe; not-a-first line acknowledging the existing German tradition, including the one associated with Rudi Paret), Inhaltsverzeichnis, Teil I · Die Übersetzung, 114 sections with the Arabic surah name, an italic intro line, basmalah Arabic + italic fixed-formula line "Im Namen Gottes, des Wohltätigen, des Barmherzigen." in the 112 surahs that carry the frame (surah 1 counts it as 1:1; surah 9 has none), the translator's note inside 5:6 with its continuation after it ("Manche Lesarten setzen das Wischen bis zu den Füßen fort; die hier gedruckte Lesart sagt waschen."), and 15 prostration markers (11 *[Niederwerfung — empfohlen]*, 4 *[Niederwerfung — verpflichtend]*) after the verse's Arabic line.
- Quran-alone discipline: no hadith/tafsir material imported, no traditional specifics added, no percentage for zakat.
- The audit is **structural** (counts, sequences, the Arabic character-for-character, markers, residue scan), as for the other drafts of this project: it is not a verse-by-verse philological proof. Final language quality requires a native German reviewer.
