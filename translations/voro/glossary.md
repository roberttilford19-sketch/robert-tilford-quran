# Võro lane — glossary of seated key forms

All forms below trace to the harvested inventories
(sources/giella-lemmas.json, sources/wordfreq-forms.json,
sources/kaikki-vro.jsonl) as screened by verify.py.

- **Jumal** — God (nom.); **Jumala** — of God (gen., attested in
  corpus forms and as the stem of GiellaLT compounds such as
  jumalakoda, jumalasõna); **Jumala nimeh** — in the name of God
  (nimi, name; nimeh, illative, the fixed Basmalah form).
- **Arm** — mercy / grace; both Basmalah mercy names seat on this
  single attested stem (disclosed single-stem seating, BUILD-SPEC).
- **ingli** — angel. **saadik** — messenger (the form seated where
  no compound "sõnumitooja" could be verified). **prohvet** —
  prophet (corpus-attested).
- **raamat** — book. **sõna** — word. **märk** — sign.
- **tii** — path / way. **õige** — straight / right.
- **taivas** — heaven / sky. **maa** — earth / land. **ilm** —
  world. **päiv** — day / sun. **öö** — night (corpus form).
- **elo** — life. **surm** — death. **hing** — soul.
  **vaim** — spirit. **süä** — heart. **iho** — body.
- **inemine** — person / human. **miis** — man / husband.
  **naane** — woman / wife. **latse** — child (stem form).
  **esä** — father. **imä** — mother. **veli** — brother.
  **sõsar** — sister. **poig** — son.
- **usk** — faith / belief. **patt** — sin. **kohus** — judgment.
  **palvõ** — prayer (noun; the verb "to pray" had no verified
  seat and stands as a GAP). **armastus** — love.
- **kiitmä** — to praise. **avitama** — to help.
  **juhatama** — to guide. **ütlemä** — to say.
  **kutsma** — to call. **tiidmä** — to know.
- **Koraan** — the Qur'an: attested as a Võro word in Wiktionary
  (cited in the candidate brief); in this draft the Master's form
  "Quran" is carried under the names policy, and Koraan is
  recorded here as the attested Võro title form for the checker.
- Case-marker words (disclosed device): **lõ** (to), **h**
  (in / into), **gaq** (with), **st** (from), **man** (at / by),
  **pääl** (on), **jaos** (for).
- Numbers: **üts** 1, **kats** 2, **kolm** 3, **neli** 4,
  **viis** 5, **kuus** 6, **säidse** 7, **katõssa** 8,
  **ütessä** 9, **kümme** 10, **sada** 100, **tuhat** 1000.
