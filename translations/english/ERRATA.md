# Errata — Ayah Companion, English Edition

## 6:86 — "Elijah" corrected to "Elisha"

*Erratum entered 2026-11-16, under Robert Tilford's Decision 36 ruling (RULED A): that the English edition carry its correction in the open, where its readers meet the text.*

**The seat.** Verse 6:86, in the list of the prophets God favored.

**What earlier printings read.** "And Ishmael, and Elijah, and Jonah, and Lot—We favored each one of them over all other people."

**What the text now reads.** "And Ishmael, and Elisha, and Jonah, and Lot—We favored each one of them over all other people." The verse text as it now stands in the master is the corrected text.

**The ground.** The Arabic of 6:86 names al-Yasaʿ — Elisha — a different prophet from Ilyās (Elijah), who stands in his right seat at 6:85. At 38:48 the text has always printed Elisha correctly. The error was a name-slip in the English; there is no variant in the Arabic.

**How it was found.** The catch belongs to The EYE of Robert Tilford's Grok audit crew. It was relayed by GrokBot, carried to the desk by Robert's own hand, and verified against the master before any ruling was sought.

**The ruling and the correction's course.** Robert Tilford ruled the correction (Choice A) on 2026-11-14 — "I'm calling it — let's bring it forward" — and the master was corrected the same day: the verse itself, and the word-index entry for "elijah" (frequency corrected from 3 to 2; 6:86 removed from its verse list, which now reads 37:123, 37:130). The correction then ran through the translation lanes as a counted, verified pass, closing at 108 of 108 lanes when the one held seat, Thai, was settled by Robert's ruling on 2026-11-15. The last stale working copy — the Jamaican lane's English slice at this verse — was closed under Decision 19 on 2026-11-16. The full record of the correction stands in `MASTER-CORRECTION-ADDENDUM-2026-11-14.md`, kept beside the master.

**Scope, stated plainly.** The locked PDF shelved in this folder, `Quran_Recital_Tilford_ENGLISH_LOCKED_6236.pdf`, was typeset from the pre-correction master and still prints "Elijah" at 6:86; it carries the error until a future edition, and this erratum is the note that stands beside it.
