# FLAGS ADJUDICATION — Navajo lane, 2026-11-24 (completion wave)

*FLAGS-NV.md itself is untouched. This dated layer adjudicates its bullets against the
grounding record finished by the 2026-11-24 completion wave: the merged concordance
(`wave/concordance-full-wave-nv.json`), which carries 686 receipted forms — 191 lane
receipts from the lane's own records and 495 published-citation receipts added by the
wave (Wiktionary Navajo as extracted by Kaikki.org; Young & Morgan, The Navaho Language,
U.S. Office of Indian Affairs, 1943) — covering 41,747 of the manuscript's 64,894 word
occurrences (64.3 percent). The remaining 9,757 forms stand in the wave's gaps register
with the reason no attestation was found.*

## What this layer does, and what it does not do

This is a **form-attestation adjudication**. For each flag bullet it takes the Navajo line
the bullet quotes, tokenises it by the concordance's own rule, and asks one question of
every distinct form in the line: does the finished grounding record now attest it? It
answers only that question. The bullets' other questions — whether a rendering carries
its verse's meaning, whether a compression is faithful, whether a phrasing is fluent —
are judgements no dictionary can take, and they stay open exactly as FLAGS-NV.md left
them. Nothing in this layer is framed as awaiting native review: under Robert Tilford's
ruling of 2026-11-18 the desk's verification against published sources is the standard,
and what the published record cannot settle is recorded as open, with the reason.

## The counted result — 4,459 bullets adjudicated

The bullets are those standing in FLAGS-NV.md in its standard verse-level format
(**ref** | severity | Navajo line | concern | Arabic). Of the 4,459:

- **Settled on attestation — 1,155.** Every distinct form in the bullet's quoted Navajo
  line now carries a receipt. The bullet's attestation question is answered; its
  semantic and fidelity questions, where it raised them, remain as written.
- **Partly settled — 2,485.** Some forms in the line are now attested and some are not:
  1,201 bullets gained at least one published-citation receipt from the wave, and 1,284
  rest on lane receipts for part of the line with the rest still unattested.
- **Confirmed ungrounded — 814.** No form in the bullet's line carries any receipt; the
  flag's caution stands confirmed by the finished record, and the forms stand in the
  gaps register as examined-and-unattested.
- **No checkable form — 5.** The quoted line contains no token that stands in the
  concordance (structural or intro lines).

Per-bullet detail (bullet reference, by category) is computed in
`wave/flags-adjudication-nv.json`, generated by `wave/rebuild-nv.py` from the merged
concordance, so any checker can recompute this layer's counts exactly.

## The standing remainder

The draft's deepest pattern is untouched by any grounding wave and is stated here as
plainly as FLAGS-NV.md states it: the translation leans on transliterated Arabic for
much of its sacred and connective vocabulary — 3,826 of the 9,757 gap forms are Arabic
transliterations or proper names, outside the Navajo dictionary record by design — and
on inflected Navajo verb forms that no published dictionary lists one by one. Settling
those is translation work, not grounding work, and it is Robert's to commission.
