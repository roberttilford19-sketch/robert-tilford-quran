# GLOSSARY-NV.md — Navajo First-Draft Glossary

**Status: review draft — NOT for publication.** Terms below are the provisional renderings locked for the first draft. Every one needs native Diné bizaad speaker review. Where the Quran defines a term, the Quran's own usage governs (Quran-alone rule: no hadith, sunna, or traditional details added — e.g. *zakat* carries NO percentage because the Quran gives none).

## §5. Proper names (transliterated from Arabic, no exceptions)

Adam, Nuh, Ibrahim, Ismail, Ishaq, Yaqub, Yusuf, Musa, Harun, Dawud, Sulayman, Ayyub, Yunus, Ilyas, Alyasa, Zakariya, Yahya, Isa, Maryam, Muhammad, Hud, Salih, Shuayb, Lut, Talut, Uzayr, Luqman, Imran, Firawn, Haman, Qarun, Yajuj, Majuj, Quraysh, Thamud, Aad, Madyan, Saba, Tubba, Abu Lahab, Dhulqarnayn.
Peoples/places transliterated simply (Makkah, Madinah, Misr). Other names transliterated from Arabic at first occurrence and flagged (see FLAGS-NV.md: e.g. Dhul-Kifl, Malek, Zayd, Wadd/Suwa/Yaghuth/Yaquq/Nasr).

## §6. Fixed renderings

- Basmalah: *Allah bízhiʼ bee, Arrahman dóó Arrahim.*
- Verse-initial "Say," → `Allah bizaad:` (speech heading) with quoted speech
- Meccan → Makkahdę́ę́ʼ · Medinan → Madinahdę́ę́ʼ
- Prostration notes kept verbatim in English: `*[prostration — recommended]*` / `*[prostration — obligatory]*`
- Muqatta'at (disconnected letters) copied as-is (e.g. "Alif, Lam, Meem.")
