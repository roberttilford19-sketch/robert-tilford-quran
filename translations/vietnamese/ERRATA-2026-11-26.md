# ERRATA — Vietnamese lane, 2026-11-26

## Eight verses corrected under Robert Tilford's ruling A

**How this arose.** On 2026-11-25 Robert raised the pivot question: most lanes on this shelf, this one included, were rendered from the Locked English Master rather than from the Arabic, and he directed an audit of what that cost. On 2026-11-26 the desk completed stage two of the widened pivot audit (record: `~/workspace/.tools/pivot-audit/WE-SEAT-ALL-LANES.md`): all 1,045 verses where the Arabic speaks of God in the plural and the master renders "We" were carried through every lane that marks number in free-standing words. In this lane, eight verses carried the seat with the bare singular divine pronoun **Ta** where the Arabic is plural and the lane's own convention for the divine We is **Chúng Ta** (used 821 times in the manuscript; the possessive của Chúng Ta stands 129 times).

**The fairness note, carried on the record:** Ta is also Vietnamese's reverential pronoun for God, so whether these eight were errors or a register choice was served to Robert as his call, not silently corrected. **Robert's ruling (2026-11-26, by his tap of A and his word in chat):** correct all nine confirmed bent seats across the two affected lanes, these eight among them. His ground, in his words: "I've gotta respect the Arabic. We gotta be true to source."

## The corrections

In each verse only the pronoun moved, Ta → Chúng Ta, at the seat or seats where the Arabic is plural. Nothing else in any verse changed.

- **11:10** — *Và nếu Ta cho người ấy nếm…* → *Và nếu **Chúng Ta** cho người ấy nếm…* (Arabic *adhaqnāhu*, "We let him taste").
- **12:24** — two seats in one verse: *Đó là cách Ta chuyển điều xấu…* → *Đó là cách **Chúng Ta** chuyển…* (Arabic *li-naṣrifa*, "that We might turn away"); and *tôi tớ trung thành của Ta* → *của **Chúng Ta*** (Arabic *ʿibādinā*, "Our servants").
- **13:36** — *Những người mà Ta đã ban Kinh Sách* → *Những người mà **Chúng Ta** đã ban Kinh Sách* (Arabic *ātaynāhumu*, "We gave them").
- **35:31** — *Những gì Ta đã truyền cảm hứng cho ngươi* → *Những gì **Chúng Ta** đã truyền cảm hứng cho ngươi* (Arabic *awḥaynā*, "We revealed").
- **37:81** — *tôi tớ có đức tin của Ta* → *của **Chúng Ta*** (Arabic *ʿibādinā l-mu'minīn*, "Our believing servants").
- **37:111** — the same formula, the same correction.
- **37:132** — the same formula, the same correction.
- **37:171** — two seats in one verse: *Lời của Ta đã đi ra trước cho các sứ giả tôi tớ của Ta* → *Lời của **Chúng Ta** đã đi ra trước cho các sứ giả tôi tớ của **Chúng Ta*** (Arabic *kalimatunā*, "Our word"; *ʿibādinā*, "Our servants").

Counted plainly: eight verses, ten pronoun seats (12:24 and 37:171 carry two each).

## Verification

After the corrections: the manuscript still holds 6,236 verse markers. A diff against the pre-edit state shows these eight verse lines and nothing else.
