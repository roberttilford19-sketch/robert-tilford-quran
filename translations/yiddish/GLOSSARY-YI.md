# Glossary — Ayah Companion, Yiddish (ייִדיש)

Locked terminology of the Yiddish lane, in standardised YIVO orthography (Hebrew script). Name forms and theological vocabulary follow the control text, Yehoash's *Toyre, Nevi'im, u-Ksuvim*; lexical standard: Harkavy's dictionaries (1898/1928). Weinreich 1968 was not consulted (borrow-only) and is not claimed.
