# Tundra Nenets lane — glossary of fixed seats (2026-11-28)

Every seat below is attested in a named Tundra source: **IBT** = the
IBT Four Gospels and Acts (2025), incl. its printed glossary Вадиˮ
хамэдиˮ (fact and citation only); **G** = the GiellaLT dictionary data
(work/lexicon-giellalt.json, lemma + paradigm class of record);
**INEL** = the INEL Nenets Corpus Tundra subset; **UDHR** = the UDHR
text (lect verified Tundra). Strata: TRAD traditional · ORTH Orthodox
usage as attested in IBT · RUS Russian-mediated, naturalised in the
attested sources · DEV disclosed descriptive device built from
attested parts. Inflected forms follow each lemma's paradigm class;
a regular paradigm cell of an attested lemma is attestation, not
invention.

## A. God, heaven, the unseen
- **God — Нумʼ** (G "sky"; IBT glossary s.v. Нумʼ "Бог"). TRAD name of
  the supreme being, seated by IBT for God. Capitalised as the Master
  capitalises. Oblique stem Нувʼ- in inflection (IBT passim).
- **Lord — Нумʼ Ерв** (IBT); Ерв alone "lord, master" (IBT).
- **heaven / sky — Нумʼ, Нувʼ** (IBT, Matt 6:9). The heavens and the
  earth: Нумʼ, Я (see B).
- **Kingdom (of God/Heaven) — Параӈода** (IBT: Нувʼ Параӈодадава
  "Kingdom of Heaven", Matt 5:3); king — параӈг family (IBT); хороль
  (G, RUS) only where the Master means an earthly king in narrative
  and параӈг does not fit — log the choice.
- **Throne — Параӈодаʼ сидяӈг** DEV (сидяӈг "seat" attested IBT;
  genitive construction on attested parts). Log every seat.
- **angel — Нувʼ юне ядэлана** (IBT glossary; DEV phrase fixed as the
  standing seat: "heaven's walking news-bearer"; ядэрць "walk" G,
  юне "news/message" IBT, ядэлана participle IBT). Plural by the
  participle's regular plural.
- **Holy Spirit — Хэбидя Индˮ** (IBT). **spirit / soul — Индˮ** (IBT;
  G "breath"). **holy — Хэбидя** (IBT, G).
- **prophet — Нумʼ Вади вадета**, pl. Нумʼ Вади вадетиˮ (IBT, Matt
  5:12; "one who tells God's word"). **messenger** (general) —
  ңэдарабэй (G), ңэвана (G); **to send — ңэдарась** family (G).
- **revelation / God's word — Нумʼ Вада** DEV on IBT pattern (Вада
  "word, language" G/IBT); the Qur'an as **the Book — Падарˮ**,
  capitalised as a title where the Master means the Qur'an itself
  (Падарˮ "book, writing, paper" IBT, G). **Scripture — Хэбидя
  Падарˮ** (IBT).

## B. World, life, death
- **earth, land — Я** (G). **world — Яндирˮ** (G, "maailma").
  **the worlds** (Master "Lord of the Worlds") — яндирˮ in the
  plural/possessive forms the paradigm gives; log the seat at 1:2.
- **life — илʼ** (IBT, G илесь "live"). **eternal life — Малсяда
  илʼ** (IBT phrase). **death — хаˮма** (IBT); **to die — хась** (G).
- **light — яля** (IBT, John 8:12; also "day" G — context decides,
  disclosed). **darkness — пэвва** (G). **sun — хаерˮ** (G).
  **moon — ирий** (G). **star — нумгы** (G). **sea — ямʼ** (G).
  **water — иˮ** (G). **fire — ту** (G).

## C. Faith, sin, mercy
- **faith — Пунрёбцо** (IBT). **to believe — пунрёсь** (IBT stem
  пунрё-; G пунрёсь/пунрась). **believer — пунрёда** (IBT participle,
  attested with full inflection). **unbeliever / disbeliever —
  ни пунрёда** DEV (negative participle pattern attested IBT:
  нисянанаˮ "the unforgiving/unmerciful" Matt 6:15 context).
- **sin — Хэбяха** (IBT, G). **sinner — хэбяхасавэй** (G).
  **evil — вэва** (G adj), **вэнзу** (G; IBT Вэнзу индˮ "evil
  spirit"). **good — сава** (G).
- **mercy, grace — сянава** (IBT glossary "благодать"). **merciful —
  сянана** (IBT, Matt 5:7 Сянанаˮ pl.). **to have mercy — сяна-**
  verb stem (IBT). **the Merciful** (epithet) — Сянана.
- **repentance — Хэбяхамʼ хаева** (IBT phrase). **forgiveness —
  хаева** (IBT); **to forgive — хаˮавра-** (IBT verb, Matt 6:12).
- **prayer — Туёˮма** (IBT). **to pray — туё-** (IBT verb stem);
  also хынумʼ (G noun), нянмбась (G verb) where register fits — the
  standing seat is the туё- family.
- **truth — ненэся** (IBT John 14:6; G noun). **true — ненэся**.
  **lie — сиева** (G); to lie — сиерась (G).
- **fear (of God) — пин** (G noun); **to fear — пилюць** (G).
- **love — мэнева** (G noun); **to love — мэнесь** (G).
- **peace — Таӈгабцо** (IBT); also ңэвадярˮ (G).
- **blessing — Сава вади мэˮма** (IBT phrase, "giving of good
  words") DEV as fixed phrase; **to bless** by the same phrase's
  verb мэ- "give" (G мэсь).
- **sign — пуномдˮ** (G); also марка (G, RUS) — standing seat
  пуномдˮ. **reward — недбя** (G); also манзараˮма (G).
  **punishment / torment — маяӈг** (IBT "suffering, torment");
  **to punish — танарась** (G).

## D. Judgment, the last things
- **judgment — судеӈгова** (IBT; RUS stratum stem суд-, naturalised,
  disclosed). **judge (noun) — судеӈгода** (IBT); **to judge —
  судеңгось** (G). **Day of Judgment — судеӈговаʼ яля** DEV
  (яля "day/light", genitive construction). **Master of the Day of
  Judgment** (1:4) — судеӈговаʼ яляʼ Ерв DEV, logged.
- **resurrection — Яӈорʼ илелоˮма** (IBT phrase).
- **paradise — Ябдана я** (IBT glossary "рай"; ябдана "happy,
  blessed" IBT/INEL). **garden — сад** (G, RUS) for earthly gardens;
  paradise always Ябдана я.
- **hell — Яʼ ӈылы** (IBT glossary "ад", "beneath the earth");
  **Gehenna — Геенна** (IBT) where the Master carries the name
  sense. **Satan — Сатана** (IBT, name form as in the Master).
  **devil — Ӈа** (IBT glossary). **idol — хэхэ** (G).
  **priest — хэхэдаӈгода** (G/IBT; TRAD/ORTH).

## E. Practice
- **fasting — Нива ӈавормамʼ мэˮма** (IBT glossary phrase for пост,
  "the not-eating of food") DEV as the fixed phrase; the verb by the
  same periphrasis. **alms — ӈэвасэй** (IBT Matt 6:2–4; G);
  **to give alms — ӈэвасэймʼ мипа-** (IBT phrase; мипа- "give").
  Zakāh rides ӈэвасэй, disclosed here: the Master's "alms" and
  "obligatory charity" both seat ӈэвасэй.
- **pilgrimage (ḥajj) — ⟦GAP: pilgrimage⟧** standing. No attested
  Tundra term in any harvested source.
- **Sabbath — суббота** (IBT token; RUS stratum, disclosed).
- **sacrifice — маядярˮ** (G noun); **to sacrifice — ханомбась**
  (G). **baptism — Хэхэдаӈгова** (IBT) — for the Qur'an's ṣibghah
  seats (2:138) render the Master's sense ("God's dye/colouring")
  by the attested "colour" word if the paradigm allows, else log a
  device; never seat Хэхэдаӈгова silently there.
- **to bow / prostrate — туйкумзь** family (G "kumartaa");

- **witness — хамэди** (IBT/INEL "evidence, testimony") DEV for the
  noun; the person by манэда "one who has seen" DEV (манэць "see") —
