## The gaps, term by term (marker count)

- **jinn — 32 markers.** No attested Upper Sorbian word for the
  jinn was found in any harvested source (dźin was tested; the
  apparent occurrences resolve to other words). Every seat stands
  open as ⟦GAP: jinn⟧, the rest of the verse rendered in full.
- **hypocrites — 26 markers; hypocrite — 6; hypocrisy — 3.**
  hypokrit, dwójličny, wobłudnik, pokrytec were all tested; none is
  attested. The seats stand open.
- **adultery — 14 markers.** cudzolózstwo and variants tested;
  not attested. The seats stand open, carried grammatically by
  attested carrier verbs.
- **polytheists — 10 markers.** połyteista is a Polish-shaped
  form, untraced; mnohobóžny likewise untraced. The seats stand
  open behind a descriptive clause on attested stems (the device
  first used at 10:105 and 12:108).
- **pilgrimage — 9 markers.** hadźija and putowanje are not
  attested; the rite stands open where its technical sense will
  not carry. "Pilgrims" in the general sense is seated on putnik
  ("traveller", attested), disclosed here; two seats (3:97, 9:3)
  are rendered descriptively and were passed by the checkers as
  disclosed descriptive seats.
- **usury — 8 markers.** lichwa tested; not attested. Open.
- **she-camel — 7 markers.** wjelbłud "camel" is attested (Soblex;
  running text) but no feminine form traces; the formations the
  translators first wrote (kamelica, kamelka, wjelbłudnica,
  wjelbłudźica) were struck by the first checker, and the seats
  now carry the marker with the descriptive carrier "žónski
  wjelbłud" (the device first used at 11:64).
- **wombs — 1 marker (31:34).** The formation wućisćenca was
  struck by the first checker as untraced; the seat stands open.
- **blasphemers — 1 marker (86:17).** No attested noun found;
  the seat stands open rather than substituting a different word.

## Descriptive devices (disclosed)

- **Zakāh** is rendered by the descriptive device dar chudym
  ("a gift to the poor") and dać-phrasings: jałmužna "alms" is not
  attested in any harvested source. No percentage is stated
  anywhere (Rule A).
- **All-Powerful / Almighty** is carried by móc-phrasing
  ("kiž ma wšu móc" — "who has all power"): the adjective
  wšomócny is NOT attested and is not used; the translators'
  initial seats of it (35) were struck by the first checker and
  re-seated on the phrasing.
- **Provision** (the zaopatŕ- seats) is carried by the attested
  zastar- family after the normalised stem traced nowhere; the
  substitution is disclosed in the first remediation log and was
  upheld by two checker passes (the family already carried the
  Master's "Sustainer" at 2:255 before remediation).
- **"Average" at 5:89** is carried descriptively ("ze srjedźneje
  měry toho") on the attested srjedźny and měra; the Czech-shaped
  průměra was struck by the second checker.
- **"By force" at 20:45 and 21:80** is carried descriptively
  (móc-phrasing; "před wašimi nadpadami") after the nasil- stem
  was proven untraced in all six inventories.

## The base, honestly

Modern literary Upper Sorbian (hornjoserbšćina), Latin script,
the orthographic standard of 1948, Bautzen-based literary norm.
The Bible control was the public-domain complete Bibles
(1728–1905), fact and citation only; the modern Catholic Bible
(Domowina-Verlag, 2006) is in copyright, was not consulted as a
base, and no approach was made to its publisher — the lane
brief's settled boundary, observed. The checker passes were made
against the same public sources that supply the attested base;
the checkers were independent of the builders, not of the norm's
sources. That overlap stands disclosed here and in the book's
front matter.
